# 22 September 2026 | A pilot without a reliable denominator
The [22 September experiment note](../minutes_note/2026-09-22.md) records that the camera and IR illuminator had been assembled. The pilot reported roughly 66% false rejects, but did not record the trial count, threshold or capture sessions. The team therefore could not use that figure as a performance estimate. Background shielding and a camera comparison were proposed, not demonstrated.
