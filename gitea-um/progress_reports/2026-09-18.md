# 18 September 2026 | First acquisition workflow
The [18 September minutes](../minutes_note/2026-09-18.md) record working acquisition on a Raspberry Pi 4 with a NoIR Camera V2 under ambient light. The landmark pipeline estimated palm orientation and scale and generated a 128 × 128 grayscale region. Even when tracking succeeded, crop position and scale shifted between captures of the same palm. Full system performance had not been validated.
