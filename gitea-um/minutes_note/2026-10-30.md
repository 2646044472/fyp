## 1. Basic Demonstration Pipeline

- Measure the reliable working distance using a ruler and document the acceptable operating range.
- Design an enclosure that supports consistent camera placement and capture geometry.
- Increase the participant sample size, including both registered and unregistered users, to support open-set evaluation.
- Analyse genuine and impostor scores under 1:N identification. Select acceptance and rejection thresholds using a separate calibration set, then evaluate them on test data.

## 2. PalmIR Recovery Network

**Current observation:** Image recovery has produced little difference in recognition performance.

**Proposed diagnostic experiment:**

- Apply controlled artificial blur to palm images, then perform recovery.
- Compare recognition performance before degradation, after degradation, and after recovery.

**Concern:** Artificial blur experiments may have limited relevance to practical deployment.

**Alternative application scenarios:**

- Dirt on the palms of construction workers.
- Partial occlusion caused by adhesive bandages.

These scenarios should be evaluated separately, as blur, dirt, and occlusion involve different types of image degradation.
