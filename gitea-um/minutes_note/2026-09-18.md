## 2. Recent Main Research Progress: ROI Stability Investigation

We observed that even when the system reports successful ROI tracking, the same palm still shows positional and scale differences across different samples.

Accordingly, we performed a code analysis of the existing `palm-detector-mcp-v2` and an offline geometric reconstruction of ten ROIs.

Issue Identified:

The current ROI width and height are both set to twice the MCP span. When the ROI extends beyond the camera frame, the program automatically shrinks the entire crop region and then resizes it back to 128×128.

As a result, different hand positions within the frame can produce different palm-to-ROI proportions in the final ROI. This is a confirmed geometric consistency issue.

However, it has not yet been proven to be the sole cause of recognition errors, nor has its impact on Fast-CC accuracy been quantified.

In addition, the investigation found that the existing quality gate can check stability within a single capture, but cannot guarantee consistent ROI scale across different captures.

## 3. Next Steps

Step 1 — Improve ROI Consistency

Using the ten saved original images and detector keypoints, compare the current 2.0×2.0 ROI with smaller crop sizes offline.

Analyze which parameters can reduce boundary fitting while preserving sufficient palm texture information. The formal pipeline will not be modified yet.

Step 2 — Unify the Recognition Pipeline

Integrate the improved dynamic ROI into Fast-CC, ensuring that enrollment and verification use the same image preprocessing and recognition method.

Subsequently, test the genuine / impostor score distributions, FAR, and FRR.

Step 3 — Complete NIR and System Testing

Add 850 nm IR illumination and check ROI quality and recognition performance under different lighting conditions.

Then test the entire pipeline from palm recognition to payment completion, covering scenarios such as unknown users, misrecognition, and payment failures.
