# FYP Meeting Minutes | November 17, 2026

**Project:** Biometric Palm Recognition
**Date:** November 17, 2026

## White-background cutouts: what did the comparison show?

The question was whether removing a cluttered background helps the system locate the palm. The comparison used the same photos twice: first locate the palm in the original image; then repeat localization on a white-background cutout. In both cases the recognizer received a crop from the **original photo**, not the cutout, so that segmentation could not replace the palm texture.

The recorded pilot completed by this date produced the following counts. An output means the pipeline located a region and encoded it; it does not mean the crop was correct or the hand was recognized.

| Photo set | Planned | Original → output | White background → output |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 11K Hands, palm-side engineering check | 12 | 12 | 11 |
| X-Palm smartphone, two photos per condition across six conditions | 12 | 12 | 11 |

The missing X-Palm output was a far-distance case. Parts of the fingers and wrist edge disappeared in the cutout. Some original-image crops also included finger bases or wrist edges. The small comparison therefore gave no reason to make white-background localization the default. It did not include an enrollment gallery, so it measured neither identity accuracy nor a false-accept rate.

## Follow-up discussed

- **Finish the comparative evaluation.** Keep the photo set fixed across methods. Report failed crops separately from wrong matches when comparing recognition on the same enrolled hands. The small pilot above is completed work, not the full comparison.
- **Improve the region of interest (ROI).** Check whether the crop actually contains a consistent patch of palm across distance and hand pose. Inspect its center, size and orientation rather than counting every returned box as a success.
- **Try video capture.** Explore whether a short sequence gives a more stable palm crop than a single still. This was a proposed trial; no video result or validated improvement was reported at this meeting.
