# 21–27 September | A pilot without a reliable denominator
The [22 September experiment note](../minutes_note/9-22.md) records that the camera and IR illuminator had been assembled. The pilot reported roughly 66% false rejects, but did not record the trial count, threshold or capture sessions. The team therefore could not use that figure as a performance estimate. Background shielding and a camera comparison were proposed, not demonstrated.

A [separate documented Fast-CC pilot](../minutes_note/2026-09-22-fastcc-pilot-public.md) used one fixed reference: four out of nine same-hand probes and one out of nine different-hand probes were accepted. These counts still do not establish a population error rate.
