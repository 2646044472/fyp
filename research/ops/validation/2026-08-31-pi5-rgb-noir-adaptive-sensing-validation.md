# Validation Audit: 2026-08-31 Pi 5 RGB/NoIR adaptive-sensing candidates

## Decision investigated

Whether the newly proposed candidates can support a research claim using the confirmed Raspberry Pi 5B (8 GB RAM, 64 GB card), official RGB camera, official NoIR camera, IR illuminator, and adapter hardware. The candidate packet proposes: ACR-MODE (adaptive RGB/NoIR recapture), ILLUM-CAUSE (active IR probe for low-light versus optical contamination), BURST-COMP (adaptive burst timing), MOD-MISSING (physical RGB/NoIR mode choice), and CAL-TRANSPORT (cross-camera threshold transport). The intended setting is a non-personal tabletop record of printed fiducials or inert materials; no production, safety, or access-control decision is assumed.

## Claim under test

The strongest proposed claim is that a Pi-local policy can choose RGB, NoIR+IR, another exposure, or `unknown` under a fixed capture/energy budget and improve the correctness-versus-cost frontier on held-out lighting and lens-condition cells. A stronger ILLUM-CAUSE variant claims that toggling IR is an intervention that identifies whether poor evidence is caused by insufficient visible illumination or optical contamination. These are Amber hypotheses only; they are not claims of global novelty.

The divergence packet also supplied three important reframings: `ASI-IR` (exact-code value of an IR capture), `PHY-MASK` (synthetic missing-modality masks versus physical camera failures), `EV-EXPO` (short exposure/burst choice against an external timing oracle), `OPT-WIT` (active IR response to a known cover state), and `FULL-CAP` (post-hoc digital corruption versus full physical capture-chain changes). They are included below; the names are not treated as evidence of distinction.

## Assumption and identification audit

1. **Camera identity and spectral response.** Raspberry Pi's Camera Module 3 product brief says the standard variant has an integrated IR-cut filter while NoIR has no IR-cut filter; both use the IMX708 and output RAW10. Therefore RGB and NoIR are not interchangeable measurements. The standard camera cannot be treated as an NIR sensor, and NoIR captures mixed visible/near-IR unless visible illumination is controlled.
2. **Two-camera timing.** Raspberry Pi's official camera software documentation says Pi 5 can connect two cameras, but when two cameras run simultaneously there is no synchronization of 3A operation. Picamera2 also states independently running cameras have no synchronization. Any claim comparing paired RGB/NoIR pixels must either use sequential captures with a static target or explicitly model motion/exposure mismatch.
3. **Lens contamination diagnosis.** A toggle experiment is not automatically an independent oracle. A transparent smudge, diffuse film, dust, or condensation can affect visible and 850-nm paths differently; an IR illuminator can itself alter auto-exposure. A contamination label must be applied before the trial (known clean/known film/known droplet), while lux and geometry are measured independently. Otherwise low-light and optical-path effects are confounded.
4. **Threshold transfer.** A threshold calibrated on one camera is a function of its spectral filter, ISP, exposure, gain, focus, and ROI preprocessing. A transfer failure is identifiable as an empirical characterization only if the camera settings, target material, and threshold freeze are pre-registered. It does not establish a transport theorem or a new classifier.
5. **Power/cost.** The Pi and cameras make capture feasible, but the candidate needs an actual current/energy measurement, IR LED drive specification, fixed geometry, and thermal/voltage logging. “Fewer captures” is not equivalent to lower energy when switching cameras or lighting incurs warm-up and ISP costs.

The additional divergence candidates do not escape these neighbors by changing the name of the endpoint. `PHY-MASK` and `FULL-CAP` are evaluation protocols whose distinction depends on a physical-failure matrix and an independent marker oracle; they are not new fusion or restoration methods. `OPT-WIT` is a contamination self-check and therefore borders the established lens-contamination detection/self-test family. `ASI-IR` is a two-action adaptive camera policy and remains covered by Lens/AdaptiveAE unless exact-code correctness plus external timing are shown to change the endpoint. `EV-EXPO` is sequential exposure scheduling with an external timing oracle; AdaptiveAE and frame-rate adaptive sensing are direct mechanism neighbors.

## Strongest simple baseline

For ACR-MODE/BURST-COMP/MOD-MISSING, use fixed auto-exposure plus a fixed periodic recapture schedule, and compare it with a single scalar brightness/blur threshold. Include `always RGB`, `always NoIR+IR`, random mode, and an oracle that chooses the best recorded option. Lens's own baselines (Auto-Exposure, Random, Oracle-Specific, Oracle-Fixed) make this minimum comparison mandatory. For ILLUM-CAUSE, the strongest baseline is a fixed two-shot protocol (IR off/on) with a simple calibrated ratio or brightness-change threshold; if the proposed learner cannot beat it at matched captures and contamination/lux cells, the mechanism is unnecessary.

## Contrarian result

The new hardware does not rescue generic adaptive sensing. ACR-MODE, BURST-COMP, and MOD-MISSING are direct instances of model/scene-adaptive sensor control, ISP/exposure scheduling, or modality activation already occupied by Lens, AdaptiveISP, AdaptiveAE, and CM-ASAP. A quality score or learned router can be useful engineering, but it cannot create an independent correctness witness.

ILLUM-CAUSE is the only candidate not immediately killed, because an IR on/off action can change the observation rather than merely transform the same frame. However, the positive claim is fragile: a physical contamination class must be independently labelled, camera auto-exposure must be disabled or logged, and a two-world counterexample (IR-transparent contamination versus ordinary darkness) can make the paired observations indistinguishable. Its honest fallback is a measured condition map or a negative result showing when IR intervention fails.

CAL-TRANSPORT is useful as an engineering preflight: freeze a threshold on RGB, test it on NoIR under controlled lux/material/geometry, and report score-shift and false-decision changes. It is not a new method unless a formally stated transport guarantee or a new calibration action survives direct audit.

`PHY-MASK` and `FULL-CAP` are the most defensible negative-result pivots. Their falsifiable claim is not “physical data are always different”; it is a finite, pre-registered rank-transfer test: train/evaluate on a declared synthetic corruption family, test on declared physical cells, and compare action/error rankings with a frozen marker oracle. A null result kills the proposed distinction but is scientifically useful as evidence that the synthetic proxy was adequate for this setup. A rank inversion is useful only as a bounded warning; it cannot support a universal statement about all cameras or fault types.

`ASI-IR` and `OPT-WIT` have the same apparatus but a weaker novelty position. Exact code equality or known cover-state labels make the endpoint auditable, yet a fixed RGB/NoIR two-shot protocol and a scalar brightness/IR-ratio threshold are very strong baselines. Ma et al. (2026) now directly occupy the passive clean-reference camera-health endpoint and camera-disjoint threshold transfer. The only residual is an active-IR incremental-information experiment, which must beat the released-style state machine and scalar baselines on held-out cover/illumination cells, not merely make the NoIR image look brighter.

## Feasibility audit

- **Hardware:** Pi 5 dual MIPI support is confirmed by official Raspberry Pi documentation, and Camera Module 3 standard/NoIR spectral-filter differences are confirmed by the product brief. Sequential switching is feasible; synchronized paired capture is not guaranteed.
- **Data:** Printed fiducials, coins, fabric, leaves, or other inert materials avoid personal data. A labelled clean/film/droplet lens fixture and a lux meter or calibrated light source are required for ILLUM-CAUSE. Public palm archives cannot provide the needed Pi target-device truth and should not be used as a substitute.
- **Compute:** Small classical descriptors, threshold routers, or a frozen lightweight ONNX model should run on Pi 5. Training RL/large ISP models on the Pi is infeasible and unnecessary; AdaptiveISP reports 100,000 iterations on an RTX 3090 in about 24 hours (appendix p. 13), so a direct reproduction is not a one-board FYP dependency.
- **Ethics:** Non-personal objects and no safety/production actions are feasible. Human subjects or biometric recording would require separate approval and must not be silently added.
- **Time:** D0 camera capture and fixed RGB/NoIR condition matrix are feasible before December 2026. A causal contamination study is feasible only after the clean/contaminated fixture, illumination control, energy meter, and threshold-freezing protocol are secured.
- **Unknowns:** Exact camera module generation, IR wavelength/power/eye-safety, adapter wiring, OS image, and dataset licenses remain [GAP] until photographed or documented. No model or dataset should be treated as deployable merely because it appears in the archive.

## Decision

- **ACR-MODE: KILL** as a thesis; it is Lens/AdaptiveISP-style sensor control. A fixed RGB/NoIR baseline can be an engineering demo.
- **BURST-COMP: KILL** as a thesis; AdaptiveAE and frame-rate adaptive sensing occupy the action and budget. Keep only as a control.
- **MOD-MISSING: KILL** as a thesis; CM-ASAP and the 2026 multimodal respiratory paper directly occupy brightness-based modality selection.
- **CAL-TRANSPORT: KILL as a thesis endpoint**. Ma et al. (arXiv:2607.14760v1, Supplementary Sec. XII) explicitly evaluate camera-disjoint threshold transfer. Retain only as a control or replication row.
- **ILLUM-CAUSE: KILL as a passive diagnosis; HOLD Amber only for an active-IR incremental-information replication test**. Ma et al. directly occupy lens occlusion versus photometric transition monitoring. Promote nothing unless IR on/off adds measurable information beyond their clean-reference statistics and a fixed two-shot ratio baseline on held-out, independently labelled cover/lux cells. Otherwise pivot to a condition map.
- **ASI-IR: HOLD (Amber, likely PIVOT)**. It is testable with exact printed-code truth, but the action/budget structure is Lens/AdaptiveAE-like and a fixed two-shot baseline is strong. Treat a positive result as a bounded value-of-information table, not a new adaptive-sensing method.
- **PHY-MASK: PIVOT (negative benchmark)**. It can test whether synthetic missing/noisy masks preserve decisions on physical disconnect/illumination/saturation cells. A rank match is a useful null; a rank inversion is a bounded reproducibility warning, not a universal guarantee.
- **EV-EXPO: KILL as a thesis / control only**. It is sequential exposure scheduling under a capture budget and collides with AdaptiveAE; an external LED/encoder oracle improves measurement validity but does not change the mechanism.
- **OPT-WIT: KILL as a generic self-check; optional active-IR replication only**. Ma et al. occupy passive lens-occlusion and photometric-transition detection with an explicit operating envelope. A fixed IR on/off ratio must beat that state machine and passive controls to reopen the claim.
- **FULL-CAP: PIVOT (negative benchmark)**. It is a sound physical-versus-digital corruption audit with a marker oracle, but it is a protocol contribution only and should not be sold as a new model or reliability guarantee.

Overall: **HOLD** only for the narrowly defined active-IR incremental-information test; all generic adaptive-camera, modality-selection, passive contamination, and threshold-transport theses are KILL or PIVOT as stated above.
