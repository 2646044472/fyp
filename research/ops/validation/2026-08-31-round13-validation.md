# Validation Audit: 2026-08-31 RGB/NoIR active-intervention candidates

## Decision investigated

This audit attempts to falsify the live candidates `IR-CAUSE`, `IR-MOD-ID`, `XFER-ACT`, and `POL-GLARE-VOI`, plus the newly proposed `LUX-AMBIG`. The assumed setup is the purchased Raspberry Pi 5B, one RGB Camera Module 3, one NoIR camera, and an IR illuminator. The intended endpoint is a non-personal printed maintenance marker with actions `retain`, `unknown`, or `reacquire`; no safety or production claim is assumed. All innovation language remains Amber until the three collision rounds and a physical pilot pass.

## Claim under test

The strongest remaining claim is narrow: on independently assigned physical cells, an additional active optical observation (IR on/off, two-frequency IR, or a clip-on polarizer) changes the *action-optimal risk ranking* beyond RGB-only, fixed RGB-to-NoIR+IR two-shot, scalar image-quality/IR-ratio rules, and always-reacquire, at equal measured capture cost. `XFER-ACT` additionally asks whether a source-calibrated action ranking survives held-out camera/lamp/material cells. None of these observations is assumed to identify a physical cause in general.

## Assumption and identification audit

1. **Two-world test [KILL risk].** Let worlds (w_0) (clean low visible light) and (w_1) (transparent cover/film) have equal RGB preview, photopic lux, blur score, and metadata. If NoIR+IR or PWM response distributions also overlap, every policy using those observations has the same conditional action distribution in both worlds. A claimed cause label or guaranteed action improvement is not identifiable. A pilot may only claim an observed finite risk difference.
2. **Illumination confounding [GAP].** Lux is photopic, while NoIR sensitivity is spectral. Equal lux does not establish equal NIR irradiance; the spectrum must be measured or deliberately fixed. LUX-AMBIG is otherwise a confounded lamp experiment.
3. **Camera-path confounding [K].** Raspberry Pi Camera Module 3 has an IR-cut filter and the NoIR variant omits it (Raspberry Pi product brief RP-008151-DS, p. 3). The pair is therefore not a synchronized multispectral camera. Pi camera documentation does not guarantee synchronized 3A across two cameras. Sequential captures can change exposure, white balance, focus, or target pose.
4. **Intervention observability [GAP].** PWM frequency is not a response measurement unless exposure windows and LED optical output are independently timed. A photodiode or oscilloscope is needed to verify modulation; a camera frame alone cannot establish phase or emitted power.
5. **Oracle leakage [KILL risk].** A decoder's success cannot be the independent label for a policy that chooses whether to decode. The printed code/template and fixture condition must supply the ground truth; otherwise the proposed method trains/evaluates on its own output.
6. **Transfer scope [K].** Four finite boards/camera paths can characterize those boards but cannot support a population guarantee over unobserved camera families. `XFER-ACT` must use the exact finite wording “held-out purchased cell/session” and report a null if ranks are preserved.

## Strongest simple baseline

The strongest baseline is a deterministic `RGB -> NoIR+fixed IR` two-shot with manual/frozen AE and WB, exact-code check, and `unknown` on any mismatch. It receives at most the same two observations as a PWM policy without modulation complexity. Additional controls are RGB-only, NoIR+fixed IR, scalar luminance/blur/IR-ratio threshold, and always-reacquire. If any of these dominates at the preregistered loss (10\,FR+1\,U+0.1\,J), the corresponding adaptive claim is killed. For polarization, compare a fixed polarizer angle and always-polarizer at matched capture delay before allowing a learned angle policy.

## Contrarian result

The literature makes the proposed mechanism more likely to be an engineering replication than a new method. Flash-Split demonstrates that an illumination pair can be essential for transparent-surface separation, while Tang demonstrates that polarization plus transparent film already reaches the same industrial QR setting. Thus a positive result that merely improves decode rate is not sufficient. The only potentially defensible result is an auditable, finite action-risk ranking that reverses on held-out physical cells and cannot be reproduced by fixed two-shot, ratio, or conservative escalation. A rank-preserving null or two-world overlap is a valuable negative boundary; it should be the planned outcome, not hidden as a failed model.

## Feasibility audit

- **Hardware [K/GAP]:** Pi 5B, RGB, NoIR and IR illuminator are available. A clip-on linear polarizer, transparent films/covers, printed markers, a lux meter and a low-cost photodiode are affordable. Exact illuminator wavelength/power, eye-safety rating, camera revision, mount geometry, and power meter remain [GAP].
- **Capture [K/GAP]:** Static targets make sequential cameras feasible. Moving or vibration claims require an independent LED/photodiode timing oracle and should not be part of the minimum thesis.
- **Labels [GAP]:** Cover state, lamp cell, material lot and contamination must be assigned before capture by the experimenter. No people or personal data are needed.
- **Compute [K]:** Marker decoding and scalar metrics run locally; no training-heavy model is required. Flash-Split/PolarFree-style diffusion is infeasible on a Pi and is explicitly out of scope.
- **Schedule [C]:** A December 2026 demo is realistic only for a static-cell pilot and deterministic controls. PWM-frequency or polarization expansion belongs after a minimal gate in 2027 H1.

## Queries and failed searches

Queries on 2026-08-31 included: `camera lens contamination modulated illumination frequency response sensor diagnosis 2024 2025`; `RGB NIR active illumination transparent film camera 2025`; `flash no flash transparent surface reflection removal 2024 2025`; `polarized transparent film QR industrial recognition 2024`; `camera action risk retain reacquire energy adaptive sensing 2025`; and `equal lux spectrum NIR camera utility`. No primary paper inspected used a Raspberry-Pi RGB/NoIR pair plus a finite equal-joule action-rank endpoint. This absence is a search boundary, not proof of novelty. Full quantitative pages for some MDPI records were rate-limited; they are not used for central claims here. No independent source was found that validates PWM frequency as an observable camera-health feature under Pi rolling-shutter exposure.

## Decision

`IR-CAUSE`: **PIVOT (Amber)**. Remove causal-diagnosis language; run only a named two-world incremental-information test. `IR-MOD-ID`: **PIVOT (Amber)**, and only after IR-CAUSE survives fixed two-shot and ratio controls; otherwise report a null. `XFER-ACT`: **PIVOT (Amber)** as a finite held-out cell/session audit, never a population transfer guarantee. `POL-GLARE-VOI`: **HOLD (Amber)** at most; Tang CVPR 2024 is a strong exact application neighbor, so it must beat fixed polarizer/two-shot and simple quality controls to remain alive. `LUX-AMBIG`: **PIVOT (negative-boundary appendix)** unless matched-spectrum cells produce a repeatable action-rank inversion. No candidate is PROMOTE.
