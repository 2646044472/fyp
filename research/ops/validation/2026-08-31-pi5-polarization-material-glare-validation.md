# Validation Audit: 2026-08-31 Pi 5 polarization, material-state and glare candidates

## Decision investigated

This audit tests the newest divergence packet's five candidates for a Raspberry Pi 5B with the owned RGB and NoIR cameras and an IR illuminator: `POL-GLARE-VOI`, `POL-WET-STATE`, `POL-STRESS-TRUST`, `POL-SEC-ANGLE`, and `POL-TRANSFER-REAL`. The proposed object is a non-personal printed marker or a fixed transparent/material fixture. The intended local action is `retain / reacquire / unknown`; no person identification, access control, safety actuation or laser experiment is assumed.

## Claim under test

The strongest positive claim under test is that a cheap, externally mounted linear polarizer (possibly rotated once) provides an edge action or material-state witness that beats fixed-filter, fixed-two-shot, RGB-only quality and simple digital baselines at equal capture/energy cost. We also test the weaker claim that paired physical and digital glare/material cells reveal a reproducible decision-risk ranking mismatch. These are empirical, finite claims; no universal statement about polarization, moisture, stress, or simulation transfer is admissible.

## Assumption and identification audit

1. **Independent truth is available only for the fixture and payload.** Dry/wet labels need delivered-water mass or a fixed film protocol; stress labels need a repeatable load/deflection reference; glare labels need cover/angle/illumination state recorded before capture. Decoder confidence is not truth.
2. **A single external linear polarizer is not a full polarimetric camera.** It cannot support full Stokes/DoLP/AoP claims without calibration. Fu et al. document interpolation, field-of-view alignment and operating-range errors for polarization imaging (PLOS ONE 2024, Secs. 2-4); Ma et al. provide a calibrated division-of-aperture method (Optics and Lasers in Engineering 2025). The pilot must use paired intensity ratios and retain the physical angle, not name a recovered Stokes quantity.
3. **Auto-exposure can erase the intervention.** Tang records auto-exposure and auto-focus in its industrial pipeline (Sec. 3.1). Pi controls must therefore be manual or logged; otherwise a lower-intensity polarizer frame may be renormalized to look equivalent and the causal interpretation is invalid.
4. **Material and illumination are confounded.** PolarFree notes dependence on scene variation, shooting angle and lighting (Secs. 1-2.2). A wet/dry ratio that changes with lamp angle or tile lot cannot be attributed to water state. Held-out lamp and specimen cells are mandatory.
5. **Two sequential cameras are not synchronized.** The Raspberry Pi documentation does not guarantee synchronized 3A behavior for the RGB/NoIR pair. Only static targets or a separately timed target are admissible; no motion or paired-frame registration claim is allowed.
6. **Security candidate has no credible benign threat model yet.** Li et al. show a modified LCD projector can create locally controllable polarizing projections against polarization vision (CVPR 2024, pp. 24706-24715; Secs. 1, 6). A phone screen is not established as an equivalent projector, and a rotating filter is not a defense against an attacker who can adapt to both angles.

## Strongest simple baseline

For `POL-GLARE-VOI`, the strongest baseline is a fixed polarizer angle selected once on a calibration card, followed by a fixed two-shot policy (`no filter` then `fixed filter`) at the same maximum captures. Add scalar ROI brightness, saturation fraction, Laplacian blur and a lightweight digital highlight mask. For `POL-WET-STATE`, compare RGB mean/variance, color ratios, saturation and fixed-lamp image differencing against a threshold on the polarizer ratio. For `POL-STRESS-TRUST`, compare RGB edge/blur/saturation and a fixed-load calibration. For `POL-SEC-ANGLE`, compare one-angle, two-angle disagreement, saturation/brightness rejection, and an adaptive attacker allowed to choose the tested projection. For `POL-TRANSFER-REAL`, compare policy rank correlation against the digital cells and a brightness/blur severity ordering.

If fixed two-shot or scalar quality is Pareto-better at equal energy and latency, the proposed action contributes no mechanism. If the outcome is exact payload validity, an always-reacquire policy is also a valid lower-false-retain control and must be reported with its availability/energy cost.

## Contrarian result

The strongest objection is that a polarizer changes the amount of light, while Pi auto-exposure, scene angle, surface material and tile/film geometry can explain the apparent gain. A fixed filter or two-shot policy may therefore match any learned/rotated policy without using a new physical signal. This objection is supported by PolarFree's ill-posed mixing model and Venkatesulu's explicit angle/background dependence; it is not resolved by the divergence packet. For security, an attacker that can observe or search both angles defeats a fixed two-angle detector, and Laser Shield already demonstrates min-energy rotation. The honest result is therefore a bounded conditional measurement or a null benchmark, not a defense claim.

## Feasibility audit

| Item | Audit result |
|---|---|
| Hardware | Pi 5B, RGB, NoIR and IR illuminator are sufficient for a static pilot. A rigid mount, inexpensive linear polarizer sheets/rotatable ring, lux meter and transparent-cover fixture are reasonable purchases. POL-WET-STATE additionally needs a pipette or measured water mass and a small kitchen scale; POL-STRESS-TRUST needs two polarizers and a repeatable clamp/weight fixture. POL-SEC-ANGLE should not buy or use a laser; a phone/LCD projection threat is not yet validated. |
| Labels | Printed payload is independent and deterministic. Cover/glare, wet/dry and load states must be assigned before capture and logged outside the image-derived policy. Without these, cause attribution is impossible. |
| Capture control | Fix geometry, manual exposure/white balance where possible, or log all camera controls. RGB/NoIR are sequential; restrict to static markers. Record lux, filter angle, frame count, latency and energy. |
| Data and ethics | Non-personal printed markers and inert plastic/tile fixtures avoid consent and privacy issues. Do not use a human hand/palm or access-control decision in this pilot. |
| Compute/time | A QR/AprilTag decoder and scalar image statistics run on Pi CPU. A 2026-12 demo is feasible; held-out lamp/specimen/fault cells and uncertainty analysis fit 2027-H1. Training a restoration or polarization network does not fit the defensible scope and is unnecessary. |
| Statistical support | A few tiles or one cover cannot support general material claims. Use block-resampled confidence intervals and hold out at least one specimen lot and illumination condition. Report the estimand as the purchased fixture family only. |

## Decision

`POL-GLARE-VOI`: **HOLD (Amber)**. It has a real static edge decision and a bounded action distinction, but Tang, PolarFree, Khandaker and Laser Shield occupy the optical components. Promote only after a preregistered pilot shows a repeatable false-retain/energy difference over fixed-filter, fixed-two-shot, scalar-quality and digital-deglare controls.

`POL-WET-STATE`: **PIVOT (Amber)**. Wet/dry polarimetry is physically plausible and labelable, but broad moisture/material claims collide with recent polarimetric road/leaf/water work and ordinary RGB baselines may suffice. Retain as a held-out-lamp finite state map or as a physical cell in `POL-TRANSFER-REAL`; do not claim generic moisture sensing.

`POL-STRESS-TRUST`: **PIVOT/KILL as thesis**. Photoelastic stress metrology and RGB/polarization load benchmarks are established. Only a bounded predictor of marker-decode failure could remain, and it must beat RGB edge/blur/saturation with repeatable load labels; no stress magnitude or safety claim is allowed.

`POL-SEC-ANGLE`: **KILL** as a mechanism thesis. Laser Shield and Li et al. directly occupy polarizer rotation and polarization attack/defense. A phone/LCD detector audit is optional, benign and high risk, not a defensible FYP direction.

`POL-TRANSFER-REAL`: **PIVOT**. Agnihotri et al. directly occupy synthetic-to-real proxy validity, so only a finite Pi-specific paired-cell ranking audit survives. It is valuable as a negative result or thesis appendix, not a positive method before observing a preregistered rank inversion.

Overall handoff: **HOLD (Amber) for POL-GLARE-VOI; PIVOT for POL-WET-STATE, POL-STRESS-TRUST and POL-TRANSFER-REAL; KILL POL-SEC-ANGLE.**
