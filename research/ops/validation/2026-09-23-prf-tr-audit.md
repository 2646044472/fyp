## Assumption and identification audit

- [KILL] A rank inversion alone closely replicates Thomas 2026's proxy-induced preferred-system reversal. PRF-TR needs a consequential action frontier beyond fixed controls, not a different board/modality.
- [GAP] Define which decoder output is retained, what physical intervention `reacquire` uses, what the second capture costs, and the human review cost. Separate empty, wrong and correct decode. A Pareto set with unpriced review is not a release decision.
- [KILL] Digital transforms of a clean image are not matched physical counterfactuals. Predeclare severity correspondence or mark unmatched cells. A hidden second *captured* frame must not be a free clean-image oracle; charge capture/ISP/decode/time/storage/energy. Payload registry and physical-cell labels cannot enter the runtime policy.
- [GAP] Three independent sessions per cell are only a feasibility floor, not power for rare false retains. Cluster intervals by session/remount, expose ties and counts, and report component frontiers before scalar-loss sensitivity.
- [E] The September 16 packet's detector-ONNX-absent statement is historical. A read-only 2026-09-23 listing finds both `palm_detection_mediapipe_2023feb.onnx` and `handpose_estimation_mediapipe_2023feb.onnx` under `code/palm_demo/models/`. Presence does not verify provenance, positive inference or Pi deployment. Palm demo artifacts are not PRF-TR marker data.

## Strongest simple baseline

PRF-TR: fixed one-shot local capture, always local second capture, always-review/abstain, frozen scalar brightness/blur/decoder-confidence threshold then one recapture, and fixed IR path if used. Charge always-on camera/IR idle energy and actual second capture. Cloud-only is infeasible for an offline story unless link, privacy and remote latency are measured; local-only is the natural control. Cell-label oracle is an unreachable upper bound. If any fixed control matches the held-out false-retain/review/capture Pareto frontier, kill adaptive/release advantage even if rank correlation differs. A-E: fixed guide + fresh still + Fast-CC + frozen profile threshold + one guided retry/unknown is the dynamic ROI/replacement comparator.
