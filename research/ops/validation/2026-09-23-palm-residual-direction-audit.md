## Claim under test

**G-MATE [C]:** For a frozen matcher, gallery and compute budget, an offline gallery-calibrated `commit top / unknown` rule improves held-out correct-enrolled completion versus unknown-person and enrolled-wrong-identity false commit beyond fixed score, quality, margin and development-calibrated gallery-specific thresholds. Cropped-file search does not estimate live transaction failure.

**VIS-GUIDE [C]:** On a frozen Pi VIS physical transaction path, an assigned prompt and at most one retake improve all-start enrolled correct completion and p95 capture-to-terminal-action time over fixed guidance, fixed quality/margin retake and conservative escalation at matched capture/deadline cost. Unknown-person wrong commits are counted with bounds, not claimed reduced. The intervention is physical; retrospective capture replay cannot reveal an untried prompt's hand trajectory.

## Strongest simple baseline

**G:** Freeze one payment-path matcher and ROI provenance. Fit a global threshold on development people; fit gallery-size- and gallery-composition-specific score thresholds using only separate development impostors where such calibration is permitted. Add fixed scalar quality floor, top-two margin and score-plus-margin conjunction; sweep thresholds on development only. Compare on identical held-out person-defined mate-present/mate-absent searches at matched unknown-commit rate, enrolled completion and Pi compute; retain enrolled-wrong-identity as a separate endpoint. Always-unknown is the zero-false-commit/zero-service bound. A gain only against the global threshold under gallery shift is killed by a simple per-gallery control. Ten-matcher consensus must pay its search cost.

**VIS:** First repair dominant capture/ROI failures on the *payment* path with fixed guide, controlled background and logged camera/exposure. Then compare fixed visible placement, fixed quality-or-margin one-retake, conservative one-retake/unknown and any proposed adaptive prompt at equal physical opportunities and deadline. Prospectively assign prompts across people/sessions; analyze all eligible starts. If a fixed guide/ROI repair or quality retake matches completion versus wrong-commit and p95 time, the research claim dies even if the demo becomes usable.

## Contrarian result

[KILL] G-MATE as a new gallery-calibration method currently has no defensible mechanism beyond palm gallery-relative scoring and fixed/per-gallery threshold controls. Offline cropped data omit capture/ROI/debit denominators. A permissioned, person-blocked calibration-failure appendix could be useful, but is not itself an edge transaction thesis. [HOLD] VIS-GUIDE is an apparatus-specific causal acquisition question only; the purported rejection has not been localized to the payment pathway and a fixed fixture correction may erase the effect. Neither residual currently supports rare wrong-debit superiority or production payment safety. No novel edge decision has survived this audit.

## Decision

**G-MATE: KILL** as an offline *novel calibration method* on current evidence; a permissioned, person-blocked baseline/non-transport audit is an optional MVP appendix. **VIS-GUIDE: HOLD** only as a prospective local causal completion/time comparison after authorized data, payment-path failure counts and stable full VIS transaction. KILL it if fixed guide, fixed quality/margin retake or ROI/fixture repair matches its held-out frontier, or if independent people/sessions and assigned prompts are infeasible. Do not reopen G from pair-count inflation, nor VIS from the debug-UI “deep learning ROI” note. A surviving edge decision would require a predeclared observation-to-action distinction, independently labeled physical episodes, a meaningful held-out person-level effect at matched capture/compute costs, simple-baseline failure and full direct-neighbor clearance including Bob Zhang and RegPalm. It may be none. Continue the mandatory demo without presenting either residual as validated security research.
