# Validation Audit: 2026-08-31 round-15 SLOW-CAP and RAW-JPEG-GATE

## Decision investigated

This audit attempts to falsify the two new systems/raw candidates for the confirmed Raspberry Pi 5B, Camera Module 3 RGB/NoIR pair and printed-marker bench. `SLOW-CAP` proposes using local CPU/memory/I/O/temperature and capture latency to decide `retain / retry / unknown` under stress. `RAW-JPEG-GATE` proposes a scalar from the same-exposure Bayer/RAW stream (saturation, linear SNR or channel occupancy) as a reliability witness beyond JPEG luma/blur and decoder confidence. The intended user is a lab maintainer recording a non-personal printed maintenance indicator; no safety, biometric, or production decision is in scope.

## Claim under test

The strongest SLOW-CAP claim is: on a fixed Pi workload and capture deadline, a telemetry-conditioned policy lowers weighted false-retain plus missed-deadline cost at matched retry/energy budget on held-out stress schedules, relative to static timeout, latency-only EWMA, image-only decode quality and always-retry controls. The strongest RAW-JPEG-GATE claim is: for one exposure, a RAW-domain scalar predicts exact-code failure at a fixed false-retain rate or improves the retain/unknown/reacquire cost frontier beyond JPEG histogram/luma/blur and decoder confidence, after accounting for RAW bytes, processing time and energy.

## Assumption and identification audit

1. **Camera raw capability:** Raspberry Pi's official camera software lists `rpicam-raw` as recording unprocessed Bayer frames and notes that raw output has no formatting metadata; `rpicam-still` is needed for DNG. The Camera Module 3 product page specifies RAW10 output. Picamera2's official manual Sec. 4.2 documents packed versus unpacked raw streams and notes that requested format/bit depth can be overwritten by the sensor mode. The purchased camera revision and selected mode must be recorded; a JPEG plus an unspecified raw buffer is not enough for a reproducible RAW claim.
2. **Storage/throughput:** Raw frames are substantially larger than JPEG and can fill a 64-GB card quickly; `rpicam-raw` documentation explicitly warns about large files and dropped frames at high rates. A raw scalar computed in memory may avoid storage cost, but the capture and unpacking cost still belongs in the energy/latency budget.
3. **Exposure/ISP confounding:** Auto-exposure, gain, white balance, black-level and digital gain alter both RAW and JPEG. Fix or log shutter/analogue gain/frame duration and disable or record 3A convergence. Otherwise a raw saturation statistic may merely rediscover an ISP exposure decision.
4. **Independent truth:** A decoder confidence score is not ground truth. Generate a known QR/AprilTag payload and verify it with an independent reference capture or payload registry. For SLOW-CAP, deadline truth requires an external LED/photodiode or monotonic trigger; process completion timestamps alone cannot show the event was captured before the deadline.
5. **Stress intervention:** CPU, memory, disk-I/O and thermal stress must be injected independently of capture and inference. If the workload itself causes all latency, “telemetry-conditioned” reduces to latency-conditioned. Thermal throttling, undervoltage and dropped frames are different fault cells and must not be pooled.
6. **No transfer guarantee:** One Pi 5 and two camera paths can support a finite characterization only. No population-level claim follows from repeated frames, and NoIR's missing IR-cut filter means RGB/NoIR are distinct observation paths.

## Candidate decisions and exact kill tests

### SLOW-CAP: **KILL thesis / PIVOT bounded replication**

Run four declared cells (none, CPU, memory/I/O, thermal/undervoltage only if safe) with the same static marker and external deadline trigger. Compare static 0.5/1/2 s timeouts, latency-only EWMA, CPU/memory/temperature telemetry, image-only decoder confidence, and always-retry. Freeze thresholds on source stress schedules; hold out stress order and lamp/material. Kill immediately if latency-only or static timeout has the same risk-cost frontier, if stress does not change exact-code error conditional on latency, or if the deadline oracle is not independent. A positive finite result remains a Pi/workload characterization, not a new timeout or active acquisition mechanism because UCC/Bian/Stubbs already occupy that family.

### RAW-JPEG-GATE: **HOLD (Amber), capability gate first**

First prove the purchased Module 3 produces a documented RAW10 stream in `rpicam-raw`/Picamera2, identify Bayer order/bit depth/black-white levels, and capture synchronized RAW plus JPEG from one exposure with fixed 3A. Then compare: RAW saturation/occupancy/SNR scalar, JPEG histogram/luma/blur, decoder confidence, combined JPEG score, and fixed reacquisition. Use exact payload truth, held-out lamp/material/camera-path cells, and report AUROC plus risk-coverage at fixed false-retain and matched bytes/latency/joules. Kill if JPEG confidence or a simple luma/blur rule ties RAW, if RAW processing/storage overhead removes the benefit, if raw mode changes exposure/timing, or if the statistic is unstable across sessions. A positive result can support only a bounded “RAW is incrementally useful for this marker family” claim; no general RAW quality metric or camera-health guarantee.

## Strongest simple baseline

For SLOW-CAP, the decisive controls are latency-only EWMA, static timeout thresholds, always-retry, and fixed periodic capture. Include queue occupancy and frame-valid metadata as lower-layer controls. For RAW-JPEG-GATE, the decisive controls are JPEG decoder confidence, JPEG luma/blur/saturation, a combined JPEG quality score, and fixed two-shot/always-reacquire. An offline oracle may choose the best recorded action but cannot be used online. Every baseline receives the same exposure, target, stress schedule, and cost accounting.

## Contrarian result

The apparent gap is mostly an endpoint change. UCC 2025 already establishes that edge resource stress breaks static timeout assumptions; Bian 2026 already provides conservative active sensing with a watchdog and shift-aware commissioning; Stubbs 2025 already documents Pi 5 queue and retention controls. Thus SLOW-CAP is unlikely to survive as a mechanism. RAW-JPEG-GATE is more experimentally distinct because it compares two representations of one exposure, but RAW's known linearity does not imply incremental decision value. For a fixed printed code, JPEG clipping/decoder confidence may be sufficient, and raw capture overhead may dominate. The honest outcome is likely a null sufficiency test, which is still useful if thresholds, exposure controls, and independent payload truth are frozen.

## Feasibility audit

- **Hardware:** Pi 5 and Camera Module 3 support RAW10 and `rpicam-raw`; Picamera2 can request raw streams. A 64-GB card is adequate for a short static pilot, but continuous RAW bursts are storage-heavy. No extra camera is needed.
- **Software:** Raspberry Pi OS Bookworm camera tools are `rpicam-*`; Python Picamera2 raw configuration and Bayer unpacking require setup time. JPEG/RAW pairing from one request must be verified rather than assumed.
- **Compute:** Scalar statistics and a QR/AprilTag decoder fit Pi 5. Training a RAW reconstruction/restoration model does not fit the scope and is already occupied by NTIRE work.
- **Stress safety:** CPU/memory/I/O stress is easy; programmable undervoltage/thermal stress can damage hardware and should be excluded from the minimum demo. Use logged synthetic load and safe current monitoring first.
- **Data/ethics:** Printed payloads and inert materials avoid personal data. Independent payload registry, external timing LED, and fixed lamp/cover cells are mandatory. No biometric or security-authentication story.
- **Time:** Raw capability preflight and baseline matrix fit the December 2026 demo. Promotion requires held-out sessions and matched-cost measurements in 2027-H1. Any missing raw mode, stable metadata, or cost measurement downgrades RAW-JPEG-GATE to an engineering appendix.

## Queries and failed searches

Queries run 2026-08-31: `Pi 5 raw Bayer rpicam-raw Camera Module 3`; `Picamera2 raw packed unpacked Camera Module 3`; `2025 RAW image quality saturation reliability gate`; `telemetry-conditioned camera decode timeout edge`; `Pi 5 queue redundant frames camera trap`; `2025 2026 RAW JPEG quality exact code`; `camera metadata exposure consistency verification 2025`.

The search found no primary paper jointly evaluating Pi 5 RGB/NoIR RAW-vs-JPEG scalar witnesses against an independently labelled exact-code retain/reacquire action. This is a retrieval gap, not novelty evidence. UCC's complete article is available through the ACM DOI record, while Rajan et al.'s full ISM 2025 text and Stubbs' poster details beyond the record description were not independently audited; claims relying on those details remain scoped. No evidence was found that a raw stream can be assumed synchronized with JPEG, nor that RAW processing is energy-neutral.

## Decision

`SLOW-CAP` is **KILL** as a thesis mechanism and **PIVOT** only to a bounded Pi stress/camera-correctness replication. `RAW-JPEG-GATE` is **HOLD (Amber)** pending a raw-capability preflight and matched-budget pilot; it must be killed if JPEG/decoder confidence or latency-only controls tie. No candidate is promoted. Overall status: **HOLD**.
