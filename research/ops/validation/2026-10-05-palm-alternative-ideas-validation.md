# Validation Audit: 2026-10-05 Palm alternatives and E-BOUND-X

## Decision investigated

Whether a new Palm-only research idea can replace or improve on conditional `E-BOUND-X`, given the user's request for other ideas and the workspace's existing gates. Finding at this audit stage: the obvious neighboring ideas are already occupied or fail identification/feasibility; the strongest remaining route is a bounded transaction-level evaluation, with a narrower user-guidance comparison as a possible fallback. This is not a novelty determination.

## Claim under test

The proposed space is contactless visible-light (VIS) palmprint recognition for a local simulated-payment workflow. The research claim must identify a specific decision and measurable difference beyond a Raspberry Pi deployment, model swap, generic quality filter, retry prompt, open-set threshold, VIS/NIR fusion, or PAD benchmark. Existing `E-BOUND-X` asks whether enrollment templates collected across separate visits improve the terminal result at an untouched later visit, after charging effort and accounting for all eligible participants.

## Assumption and identification audit

- [K] The current charter is Palm-only. Do not pivot to generic edge scheduling, model compression, or unrelated sensing simply because those topics are easier to propose.
- [K] The user confirmed that three visits can be arranged (2026-09-27 per active reconciliation). That does not establish approval, consent, retention terms, the number of returning independent people, actual fresh captures, or a completed experiment.
- [GAP] In the reviewed code inspection recorded in the 2026-09-27 packets, enrollment repeatedly reads the latest frame and saves a sample count rather than each template's frame/visit provenance. Consequently, a nominally multi-sample gallery need not represent independent presentations.
- [K] A local payment endpoint can be measured only when capture, ROI, match, identity authorization, unknown status, and terminal mock action all bind to the same eligible episode. Marginal stage rates do not recover a joint terminal-action rate without reachability/dependence assumptions.
- [K] The primary practical controls are best-quality single template, equal-count same-visit diverse templates, fixed quality/margin retake, conservative unknown/refusal, and second-VIS capture at matched effort for any missing-NIR proposal.
- [C] A surviving empirical question may be whether session diversity changes later full-path outcomes after those controls. Any expected benefit is a hypothesis; it is not established by paper future-work language.

## Strongest simple baseline

For enrollment: best-quality single visit-1 template and an equal-count, within-visit diverse visit-1 gallery, both evaluated on the same visit-3 probe at a frozen threshold. Add a conservative refusal control. For live guidance: fixed cue plus one fixed retake/quality rule. For missing-NIR claims: two VIS captures at matched time/energy. These controls can explain most apparent improvements from more templates, quality filtering, or extra capture opportunities.

## Contrarian result

The plausible “other idea” list is short after direct-neighbor review:

1. **Cross-session enrollment robustness:** this is essentially `E-BOUND-X`, already conditional and burdened by return visits, sample size, and selection effects.
2. **Open-set thresholds or gallery calibration:** generic forms collide with Palm-ID and other named direct neighbors in the existing candidate audit; simple global/per-gallery threshold, quality, and margin controls are decisive.
3. **Capture guidance / ROI repair / quality-based retry:** Palm-ID already has the main pipeline components; a fixed mount or deterministic ROI correction could remove the motivating failure before a prompt policy matters.
4. **VIS/NIR fusion, NIR dropout, or PAD:** cross-domain recognition and RGB/NIR PAD have recent direct work. NIR absence is confounded with exposure, pose, timing, or ROI unless capture is paired and physically assigned; second VIS is the obvious equal-cost control.
5. **A full-path attack study:** meaningful only with authorized physical attacks and attack lineage that actually reach the live capture-to-action path. GBU-Palm makes a broad PAD claim especially hard to defend.
6. **A transaction episode audit:** most feasible as an engineering/evaluation contribution if every eligible attempt and terminal action is linked. It can demonstrate why marginal stage rates fail, but a two-world identifiability example alone is elementary and not a new estimator.

Thus, there may be other *experiments*, but the inspected evidence does not support presenting a newly named mechanism as an already defensible thesis idea. The decision that changes the ranking is whether the team can capture enough approved, independently returning participants with reliable frame/visit lineage and a frozen transaction path.

## Feasibility audit

- **Participants/data:** three visits are reportedly schedulable, but no completion count is confirmed. The active sample-size gate shows illustrative 50–70 completers would target only very large (~20 percentage-point) net paired differences under specified discordance assumptions; a modest 10–15 point difference could require roughly 97–306. These are planning estimates, not observed Palm effects.
- **Approval:** institutional/supervisor approval, informed consent, biometric retention/deletion terms, and repeat-visit permission remain prerequisites. User scheduling availability is not approval.
- **Instrumentation:** need an atomic image/frame/timestamp/capture ID and per-template visit lineage; define the eligible-start point; log all stage failures and the terminal action on one episode record.
- **Labels:** unknown-person and authorization labels must be independent of the matcher output. App output is not ground truth.
- **Compute/hardware:** an offline equal-count template comparison is modest; extra IR capture requires the actual sensor/light path and an independent paired-capture design. PAD requires apparatus and ethics approval.
- **Time:** build freeze, approved feasibility pilot, protocol lock, longitudinal collection, and analysis are all still ahead in the reviewed records. A positive claim is therefore not yet feasible to promise.

## Decision

Do not add a replacement “new idea” yet. Keep `E-BOUND-X` as a conditional evaluation only if approval, a sufficient return cohort, fresh-frame/session lineage, a frozen matcher/action policy, and an untouched third visit can be demonstrated. If those gates fail, prefer `OPEN-ACTION-EP` as a linked all-start audit, with `STAGE-NULL` used only to state what marginal rates cannot identify. Consider `VIS-GUIDE` only after a frozen path shows capture guidance is a real bottleneck and a prospective cue comparison is possible. The alternatives inspected here are either direct-neighbor-heavy or are experiments inside these existing gates, not presently distinct methods.
