# Validation Audit: 2026-08-31 Pi 5 optical intervention, transfer, and physical-failure candidates

## Decision investigated

This audit tests the current canonical candidates that can actually use the purchased Raspberry Pi 5B (8 GB), official RGB and NoIR cameras, IR illuminator, and inexpensive optical fixtures. The supplied candidates are `IR-CAUSE`/`IR-MOD-ID`, `XFER-ACT`, `PRF-TR`/`REAL-DIG-RANK`, `POL-GLARE-VOI`, `POL-WET-STATE`, `PWR-WIT`, `LUX-AMBIG`, `ABSENCE-PULSE`, and `RS-DRIFT`. The intended application remains a non-personal printed marker or inert-material bench record with `retain / reacquire / unknown`, not a safety interlock, biometric decision, or production release.

## Claim under test

The strongest surviving claim is deliberately finite: an independently labelled physical intervention (IR modulation, polarizer angle, or power perturbation) changes the *exact-marker action-risk ranking* enough to justify one extra capture at matched joules, compared with fixed two-shot, scalar image-quality, and always-reacquire controls. A second family claims that source-device policy rankings or synthetic-corruption rankings transfer to a held-out NoIR camera/lamp or to physical faults. None is a global transfer or causal-diagnosis claim.

## Assumption and identification audit

1. **Optical paths:** Raspberry Pi's Camera Module 3 brief (RP-008151-DS, updated 2025-10-06, p. 3) states that standard has an IR-cut filter and NoIR omits it. NoIR is therefore a mixed visible/NIR path, not a calibrated NIR spectrometer. Illuminator wavelength, power, diffuser, and eye-safety remain [GAP].
2. **Timing:** Pi camera documentation states two cameras can be connected, but simultaneous cameras do not synchronize 3A; Picamera2 independently run cameras are not synchronized. Use a static target or an external LED/photodiode timing trace. Do not claim pixel-level paired motion registration.
3. **Independent truth:** QR/AprilTag decode from the same Pi frame is not an independent label. Print payload equality must be known from generation or a separate flatbed/phone reference; cover state, delivered-water mass, voltage droop, lamp spectrum and polarizer angle must be recorded before capture.
4. **Cause identification:** IR on/off is an intervention, not a guaranteed cause oracle. Auto-exposure can absorb the intervention; IR-transparent films and darkness can produce overlapping observations. The two-world overlap test is mandatory before any positive claim.
5. **Cost accounting:** A second camera, illuminator warm-up, CPU load, and recapture latency must be included in joules and wall time. “Fewer frames” is not automatically lower energy. `PWR-WIT` needs a safe programmable droop or load fixture; an inline USB meter alone cannot localize a frame-level transient.
6. **Polarization:** A clip-on linear filter provides one intensity projection. No DoLP/AoP or stress/moisture magnitude claim is permitted without calibration. Angle, geometry, clipping, and sample lot must be controlled.

## Strongest simple baseline

The required baseline set is: `always RGB`, `always NoIR+IR`, fixed RGB-then-NoIR+IR two-shot, fixed polarizer angle, fixed shortest exposure, random action, scalar luma/blur/saturation rule, Ma-style clean-reference luminance/gradient monitor, and always-reacquire. The offline oracle may choose the best recorded action but cannot be used online. Every proposed policy must match expected captures, joules, latency, and threshold-freezing rules. A candidate that only improves against a single learned router is not evidence of contribution.

## Contrarian result

The Pi purchase enables a credible demonstration but does not create an adaptive-sensing research gap. Bian 2026 is the strongest generic direct neighbor because it already separates cheap screening from expensive confirmation and reports conservative behavior under device shift. Ma 2026 is the strongest optical-health neighbor and explicitly shows camera-disjoint threshold transfer can be unreliable. PolarFree/Tang/Retinex-MEF make polarization and exposure interventions collision-prone. The only defensible positive experiment is therefore a narrow active intervention whose value is measured by an independent exact-marker decision; the more likely outcome is a null showing that fixed two-shot or scalar controls dominate. That null is useful if the cells, labels, and costs are pre-registered.

## Feasibility audit

- **Hardware:** Pi 5 plus two official cameras supports sequential static captures. A rigid mount, transparent covers, safe diffuser, polarizer and printed targets are affordable. PWM driver, lux meter, photodiode, force fixture, or programmable supply are optional instruments, not assumptions.
- **Data/labels:** Use printed payloads, inert tiles, films, water films and controlled lamps. Avoid people, palms, security access and production claims. Independent payload and condition labels are mandatory.
- **Compute:** Decoder plus scalar features/tree/logistic policy fits Pi 5. RL, diffusion restoration, or large polarization models do not fit the one-board FYP schedule and are already direct neighbors.
- **Time:** A static RGB/NoIR condition matrix and fixed baselines can produce a December demo. Active IR or polarizer promotion requires repeated sessions, held-out lamp/material cells, energy measurements and a pre-registered kill threshold before 2027-H1 optimization.
- **Risks:** Camera revision, illuminator spectrum/eye safety, manual exposure API, adapter reliability, power instrumentation, and polarizer geometry are [GAP]. Any missing item downgrades the candidate to a characterization appendix.
