## Strongest simple baseline and identification gate

For **T**, freeze matcher/gallery/threshold and the same per-episode maximum of three *physical* attempts. Compare best fixed 1, 2 and 3 capture allowances, one guided VIS retake after ROI/quality failure, calibrated top-score and top-two-margin reject, and a conservative one-shot unknown decision. Tune each on development people/sessions only. Count enrolled wrong debit separately from unknown-person wrong debit, correct completion, unknown, retries, failure to acquire and p95 capture-to-logged-debit time. An unknown-to-gallery person is not an attack presentation. A retrospective policy comparison cannot infer what a different hand-placement instruction would have produced; prospectively randomize the prompt while keeping account truth outside the matcher. The policy must improve the predeclared vector at matched attempt and resource budgets, not merely obtain higher completion by accepting more identities.

For **E**, compare random-k, best-quality frame, same-session pair consistency, k diverse templates, fixed two-session enrollment and conservative refusal at equal capture minutes, storage and gallery/search cost. Account-person binding must be externally supervised. The confirmatory later session must be distinct from both enrollment and any second visit used to choose/refuse. Include never-enrolled unknown people, refused identities, revisit time, FTE and later wrong/correct/unknown transactions in the *original eligible-person* denominator. Comparing only successfully enrolled people rewards refusal and cannot test the claim. A second visit without a third outcome visit is not a longitudinal held-out result.

## Feasibility and sample-size audit

[GAP] No confirmed roster of independent enrolled and never-enrolled people, repeat visits, research consent/ethics, storage/deletion permission, stable Pi full transaction or volume of failure/retry events. For zero wrong debits in n independent unknown episodes, the rough 95% upper bound is 3/n: 300 independent episodes only bounds about 1%, 3,000 about 0.1%. Multiple captures or repeated episodes from five volunteers do *not* give 300 independent people/instruments or production assurance. A small pilot can measure latency, acquisition failure and completion, but cannot establish improvement in a rare wrong-debit frontier. Predeclare the minimum effect and confidence width, recruit by *people and sessions*, and run a feasibility count before fitting a sequential policy. E additionally needs distinct enrollment, selection/confirmation and held-out later test visits, plus a nontrivial number of refusals/errors; this is unlikely to be rescued by more frames from one day. If wrong debits are too rare, report upper bounds and refrain from a positive security claim; do not tune threshold to manufacture errors without labeling that stress-test operating point.

## Decision

**Strongest surviving conditional candidate: T-RETRY**, only because it is executable after the VIS MVP without NIR, attacks or a third visit.
