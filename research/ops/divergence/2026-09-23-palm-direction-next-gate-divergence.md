## Top two formalizations

**A-ROI, next gate but not thesis.** [D] Unit `i` is every eligible consented physical presentation with rostered identity `Y_i`, gallery `G`, frozen threshold `tau`, assigned capture policy `Z_i`, at most `k` attempts and source-frame IDs. Let `C_i`, `R_i`, `M_i`, `A_i` denote capture, valid same-source ROI, correct thresholded match and correct idempotent mock action. [A] Counterbalance by person/session; hold camera/exposure fixed or log one-factor changes; count failed starts; do not tune held-out people. [T] Compare `P(C,R,M,A | do(Z=z))`, enrolled completion and p95 start-to-terminal time at matched captures; stage probabilities localize failure but do not prove cause without intervention. Observable: debit/unknown/retry/failure and timestamps for every start. Counterexample: debug landmark ROI succeeds while payment automatic ROI fails, or the box changes background and exposure together. Negative value: bounded failure taxonomy and MVP correction, not automatic novelty.

**G-ADMIT, conditional research lead.** [D] `G_n` is a predeclared gallery of size `n`; rostered `Y` is present or absent; score vector `s(x,G_n)` precedes action `a in {commit argmax, unknown}`. [A] Person-disjoint calibration/final rosters; all hands/crops/sessions of a person travel together; thresholds and quality rules are frozen; unknowns are independently rostered; compute budget is fixed. [T] At each `n`, compare `P(commit wrong | Y absent)` and `P(commit correct | Y present)`, separately enrolled wrong-ID, unknown and acquisition failures. Offline score/action is matcher-only, never a live debit result; live evaluation must include all physical starts and p95. Counterexample: a conservative gallery-size threshold matches the frontier, or too few unknown people make zero errors uninformative. Negative value: bounded finite-cell nontransport/null with person-level uncertainty. If lawful data or independent identities are absent, HOLD/KILL rather than manufacture sessions by file ordering.
