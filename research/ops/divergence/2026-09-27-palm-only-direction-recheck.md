## Decision investigated

Whether a genuinely new palmprint-recognition direction remains after the 2026-09-24 Palm-only scope lock, in light of the current working-tree state and primary work published through 2026-09-27. **Finding: no new candidate clears the distinct-task/action/observation bar.** Keep the existing gates as conditional experiments; do not relabel a code fix, fresh dataset, or larger evaluation as a new method.

OPEN-ACTION-EP/STAGE-NULL has better near-term scientific economy and a rigorous negative result, but only after a real end-to-end trace. No option dominates all dimensions, and none is promoted.

## Top two formalizations

### 1. E-BOUND-X — longitudinal enrollment-to-transaction evaluation

- **[D] Decision:** For each originally eligible person, choose a fixed single-session enrollment, a separately confirmed second-visit enrollment, a fixed diverse-template enrollment, or refusal.
- **[A] Assumptions:** Consent and retention approval; three disjoint stages (initial enrollment, optional confirmation, untouched later transaction); different physical presentations with frame IDs/timestamps/session IDs; one frozen matcher, gallery policy, threshold, and mock-action policy; identity/session-blocked analysis; count enrollment failures and costs from the original eligible denominator.
- **[T] Target:** A charged cross-session choice changes later all-start transaction outcome beyond equally charged best-quality, random, same-session-consistency, fixed multi-template, and refusal controls.
- **Observable outcome:** For all originally eligible people: no-template/FTE, later capture/ROI, correct identity, unknown, wrong identity, terminal mock action, and elapsed enrollment plus revisit plus transaction time; template count/storage/search cost and provenance are secondary.
- **Counterexample:** If image/template quality or fixed multi-template enrollment already predicts the later outcome, or participants who return are a selected easier subset, the apparent gain does not support the claim.
- **Negative-result value:** Directly demonstrate whether a “multi-sample” enrollment gain persists after distinct-presentation verification, person/session splitting, and burden accounting. This is not an enrollment algorithm and cannot establish a global gap.

### 2. OPEN-ACTION-EP / STAGE-NULL — episode identifiability audit

- **[D] Decision:** Determine the terminal local outcome of one eligible physical presentation: correct authorized mock action, unknown/defer, or wrong identity/action.
- **[A] Assumptions:** One episode begins before a frame is available; capture, ROI, matcher, and action reachability are recorded; identity authorization and unknown status are independently labeled; threshold and gallery are frozen; stage events are joined without dropping failures.
- **[T] Target:** The collection of marginal stage rates alone is insufficient to determine terminal wrong-action or successful-action risk whenever dependence, reachability, or stage-to-episode linkage is unspecified; linked episode observations resolve those quantities for the observed apparatus and cohort.
- **Observable outcome:** Construct pairs of episode-level joint tables with equal declared marginals but different terminal actions, then compare rate-only reconstructions with directly linked all-start outcomes on the fixed executable.
- **Counterexample:** A justified independence/reachability model may make terminal risk computable from marginals; then the audit must state those assumptions and not claim universal non-identifiability. A mock UI log without an independent authorization label cannot prove a correct action.
- **Negative-result value:** A constructive counterexample can invalidate aggregate-only safety/usability claims without inventing a new risk estimator. The implementation step is instrumentation; the scientific object is the assumption boundary and observable endpoint.

## Decision

No newly conceived candidate survives this round. Preserve the ordering already established: first freeze/trace the VIS all-start path; use E-BOUND-X only if distinct presentations, consent, and three disjoint stages can be proven; otherwise use the linked OPEN-ACTION-EP / STAGE-NULL negative audit; then consider narrow VIS-GUIDE; leave NIR-MISS-X and PA-FULLPATH behind hardware/ethics gates. New work in the checkout improves readiness only if it produces distinct frame/session lineage and a complete physical action trace. Report no global novelty claim.
