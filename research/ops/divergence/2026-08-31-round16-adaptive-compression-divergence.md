# Divergence Packet: 2026-08-31 adaptive sensing and task-aware compression

## Decision investigated

Investigate edge directions that change the observation, representation, or communication action rather than adding another RGB/NoIR selector. The focus is a Pi 5B with the purchased RGB and NoIR cameras, a static printed indicator, and an optional local network link. I searched adaptive sensing, task-aware compression, raw/JPEG representations, semantic communication, and compressed-domain inference.

## Search boundary

Searches were run on 2026-08-31 against official CVF/ECCV, ICLR, NeurIPS/arXiv, ACM/IEEE DOI records, publisher open-access pages, and author/institution repositories. Queries included:

- `2024 2025 task-aware image compression downstream machine vision edge`
- `2024 2025 progressive image transmission edge reliability task performance`
- `2024 2025 distributed image compression decoder side information camera`
- `2024 2025 compressed-domain inference image compression exact text OCR`

The candidate story uses a non-personal printed QR/AprilTag or alphanumeric maintenance indicator. A local Wi-Fi link can be emulated with a shaped link; no cloud service or production decision is assumed.

## Top two formalizations

### NOIR-SIDEINFO

- **[D]** Capture a static target with RGB frame (x) and sequential NoIR frame (s), with independent geometric registration and exact payload (y). The sender sees (x,s); the receiver gets a compressed message (b(x|s)) and may already possess (s), or receives (s) through a separately accounted channel.
- **[A]** Choose independent JPEG, ROI crop, or side-information residual coding; receiver returns `retain`, `unknown`, or `reacquire`.
- **[T]** Fix a byte/latency/energy budget (B). Compare exact-code success and false-retain risk at equal (B), with held-out cover/light/material cells. A positive result requires a confidence interval for byte reduction at fixed risk and no worse total capture cost.
- **Observable outcome:** bytes, receiver latency, Pi encode/decode time, joules, exact payload error, unknown/reacquire rate.
- **Counterexample:** if NoIR is not correlated with RGB under a cell, or if sending the decoded code (not an image) is permitted, side information is unnecessary; if sequential registration dominates, the proposed observation is invalid.
- **Negative-result value:** a null establishes that the extra camera should not be used as compression side information for this workload, constraining future multimodal-compression claims.

### PROG-RET

- **[D]** A JPEG/WebP stream is partitioned into base and enhancement chunks. The receiver observes (o_k=(q_k,\hat y_k,bytes_k,time_k)) after chunk (k), where (hat y_k) is the marker decoder result and (q_k) is a non-oracle confidence/consistency check.
- **[A]** Stop and `retain`, request one enhancement, or `unknown`. The ground-truth payload is used only after the action for scoring.
- **[T]** Evaluate fixed full, fixed low, fixed high, and progressive stop rules under shaped link rates/losses. Report expected bytes and latency at a preregistered false-retain ceiling. Do not use an adaptive learned codec in the minimum experiment.
- **Observable outcome:** risk-coverage curve, bytes/frame, p95 latency, retries, and energy.
- **Counterexample:** a low-quality base layer can produce a plausible but wrong QR payload; without an independently trusted checksum, confidence may be overconfident. If full JPEG is within the budget, progressive protocol has no value.
- **Negative-result value:** demonstrates that progressive communication is unnecessary for static markers at realistic Wi-Fi rates, or bounds when it is useful without claiming a new codec.

## Queries and failed searches

No inspected primary source jointly evaluated Pi 5 RGB/NoIR paired captures, decoder-side side information, exact printed-code success, and matched bytes/latency/energy. Searches for `NoIR side information compression`, `QR task-aware JPEG Pi`, and `RGB NIR distributed compression edge marker` returned generic multi-view codecs, smartphone/COCO/KITTI benchmarks, or product pages.

The D-CAN and ACM/CVF full texts were partially inaccessible in this session. The D-CAN abstract and bibliographic pages verify the side-information problem but not detailed ablations; the ACM/CVF records verify direct-neighbor scope but not all limitations. These gaps are explicitly unresolved, not treated as novelty evidence.

## Decision

- **NOIR-SIDEINFO: HOLD (Amber).** It is the most distinct observation/action pair in this round, but must first beat independent JPEG, ROI crop, and `send decoded code` controls at equal total capture cost. If NoIR is not available at the receiver, account for its bytes explicitly.
- **PROG-RET: PIVOT (Amber).** A deterministic progressive protocol may be a bounded systems experiment, but progressive learned codecs and task-guided communication are direct neighbors. Promote only an exact-marker stopping result, not a new semantic codec.
- **ROI-QP: KILL as a learned-compression thesis; PIVOT as a negative benchmark.** Task-aware/ROI compression is occupied; a null showing tuned JPEG/ROI dominates would be scientifically useful.
- **DCT-QR: PIVOT/KILL.** Compressed-domain inference is established and the expected Pi speedup may be too small; retain only as a low-level negative control.
- **TASK-CODEC-NULL: HOLD as a negative-result protocol.** It is feasible and honest but should not be sold as a positive method unless a reproducible, cost-inclusive win survives the simple baselines.

Overall decision: **HOLD**. The highest-value next gate is a small NOIR-SIDEINFO/PROG-RET pilot with static printed markers: measure paired RGB/NoIR registration, independent JPEG versus residual/ROI bytes, and progressive stop risk under shaped Wi-Fi. If the decoded payload or simple ROI JPEG dominates, retain that null and return to the bounded IR physical-intervention pilot rather than adding a learned codec.
