# Divergence Packet: 2026-10-06 Palm Own-Capture Failure and Next Direction

## Decision investigated

**Which Palm-only direction, if any, is justified after the Pi's new high-resolution captures still fail to separate one person's left and right palms?** The immediate decision is whether to spend effort on acquisition, ROI, matcher, or a longitudinal Palm Payment study. The twenty continuously acquired images diagnose this apparatus; they cannot validate a biometric method or a payment threshold.

## Recent-paper limitation map

- [K] **Palm-ID** already joins mobile capture, nine-keypoint homographic ROI plus thin-plate-spline alignment, learned enhancement, global/local embeddings, optional recognition-utility quality rejection, open-set search, and time-separated evaluation. Its ROI is 224×224, demonstrating that 128×128 is an implementation choice, not a universal palm requirement. It reports low-contrast/over-saturated and occluded failures and warns that hand-tuned enhancement parameters may fail across devices. Hence “raise input resolution,” “CLAHE,” “quality-based retry,” “deep matcher,” and “open-set threshold” alone have direct neighbors. Palm-ID's validation populations, models, and phone differ from this fixed Pi/NoIR system; its performance cannot be transferred by citation.
- [K] **Fan et al. 2025** analyze stand-off distance, video enrollment at multiple ranges, and edge-aware rotation-invariant ROI alignment against several alternatives. Their Discussion acknowledges palm curling and multi-palm complex backgrounds. Thus a new ROI width/center or dynamic registration is not a new mechanism here; an exact paired audit of this Pi's crop versus a manually fixed crop remains useful as fault attribution.
- [K] **X-Palm v2** provides 6,006 images from 103 people, including 81 with paired scanner and smartphone acquisition; the smartphone arm varies pose, distance, light, device and surface. Its authors manually inspect and mark five anatomical points for viable 112×112 ROIs, and report limited iterations per variation as a limitation. This directly warns against treating one person's twenty bursts as independent accuracy evidence. It also shows that human-checked ROI is a serious oracle control, not a deployable automatic solution.
- [K] **PalmBridge v1**, coauthored by Bob Zhang, explicitly targets feature shifts caused by illumination, posture, ROI errors and sensor conditions in open-set verification. **HGAIQA** (Bob Zhang coauthor) combines hand geometry, brightness/sharpness and recognition utility; **CROI/PalmALNet** (Bob Zhang coauthor) addresses missing landmarks and feature alignment. The last two records were accessible only as author/publisher abstracts, so their exact losses, limitations and protocols are [GAP]. Advisor fit is strong, but generic quality/ROI/feature alignment claims are collision-heavy.
- [E] The actual Pi diagnostic is narrower: high-resolution full still redetection produced 20/20 ROIs, yet DoN gave 7/10 rank-1 on the first-five/last-five split; same-hand median distance 0.1243 versus other-hand 0.1251; every other-hand score fell under the deployed 0.15 threshold. Central recrop fell to 5/10. The saved `white` metadata did not correspond to white paper in the frames. There is no independent person, visit, imposter roster, or controlled focus/light intervention. These facts make threshold-tuning and the recrop unfit for deployment.

## Top two formalizations

### 1. `OPT-BOUND` — the next cheap experimental gate

- **[D] Decision:** retain the current optics, change a fixed setup parameter, or abstain from recognition while recapturing; this decision is made before any simulated account action.
- **[A] Assumptions:** Same physical hands, randomized order across prespecified focus/light/distance cells, logged exposure/gain, unchanged matcher/threshold, fixed manual and automatic ROI arms, and genuinely new presentations; probe session is withheld from all tuning.
- **[T] Technical test:** For full still $I$, compare the paired score margin $m(I)=d(I,\mathrm{other})-d(I,\mathrm{owner})$, all-start ROI yield, true owner/other/unknown decisions, time and image line visibility. Conditions are interventions, not inferred causes from the old images. Include no-intervention, re-focus-only, diffuse-light-only, and their combination where physically possible. A focus chart or independently judged native palm patch should guard against using the DoN score itself as an optical oracle.
- **Observable outcome:** a predeclared cell changes held-out margins and correct/unknown outcomes, with paired confidence intervals at the participant/session unit; latency/cost is included. Ten burst frames do not supply ten independent units.
- **Counterexample:** even sharp, well lit, consistently cropped later-visit images retain overlapping DoN scores. That refutes an optics-only repair and advances `MATCH-SUFF`.
- **Negative-result value:** a bounded information-sufficiency map for the specific IMX219/NoIR distance/illumination rig; it prevents an unsupported camera purchase and separates physical acquisition failure from matcher failure. A simple lamp/focus fix makes this **engineering**, not thesis novelty.

### 2. `E-BOUND-X` — conditional research direction after the preflight

- **[D] Decision:** enroll at visit 1, request a charged confirmation at visit 2, or refuse; visit 3 is the untouched transaction endpoint.
- **[A] Assumptions:** institutional permission and informed consent; independent people and separately presented palms; atomic image/frame/visit/action lineage; threshold/gallery/code frozen before visit 3; all originally eligible participants remain in the denominator, including refusals and no-shows.
- **[T] Technical claim:** a visit-separated enrollment policy changes later all-start owner completion and wrong/unknown simulated action at equal capture/template/search cost relative to best-quality single and fixed multi-template/same-session controls. This is an estimand and evaluation protocol, not a claim to invent palm template selection.
- **Observable outcome:** paired person-level later correct action, wrong action, unknown, failure-to-enroll, total capture burden and p95 local time. Wrong-action claims need sufficient independent unknown people and honest intervals.
- **Counterexample:** fixed best-quality or diverse visit-1 templates match the confirmation arm after charged cost and original eligibility are included.
- **Negative-result value:** shows whether revisit friction buys measurable later action reliability in this bounded application. If participant count is too small, estimate only feasibility/capture stage rates and pivot to `ACTION-NULL`.
