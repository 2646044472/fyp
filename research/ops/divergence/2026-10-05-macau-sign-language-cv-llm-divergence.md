# Divergence Packet: 2026-10-05 Macau Sign Language CV+LLM

## Decision investigated

Should the FYP pivot to computer-vision plus language-model processing for Macau Sign Language (MSL), possibly on an edge device? The supplied phrase “澳门粤语” is ambiguous: spoken Cantonese and MSL are different language modalities. This packet follows the surrounding sign-language discussion and audits MSL. If the user meant spoken Macanese/Cantonese, CV is likely the wrong primary sensor; audio speech recognition plus language modeling is a separate candidate and needs a corrected task statement.

**Decision: HOLD.** The official Macau Sign Language Vocabulary Bank creates a promising, real local anchor, but its public description is a searchable lexicon with instructional videos and lexical variants, not a released continuous-translation corpus. Reuse rights, research access, independent signers, sentence-level labels, community evaluation, and a target edge platform are unresolved. CV+LLM/contextual sign translation is already a well-developed mechanism family. Do not promote “local language + CV + LLM + edge” as the contribution.

## Recent-paper limitation map

- **SCOPE (AAAI 2025; arXiv v1 2024-09-02, later conference version 2025-04-11)**: context-aware SLR and SLT. It uses conversational context in a multimodal encoder for gloss recognition and fine-tunes an LLM using prior conversational context for translation; it contributes 72 hours of Chinese Sign Language contextual-dialogue videos. This directly occupies generic “use LLM/dialogue context to improve sign recognition/translation.” It is not an MSL dataset or Macau edge deployment. Primary paper: https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/32612 ; arXiv https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.01073 . Abstract and dataset/task framing inspected; full paper was not retrievable from the AA AI download link in this pass.
- **Lost in Translation, Found in Context (CVPR 2025, pp. 8742–8752; arXiv:2501.09754)**: continuous sign video plus background-show captions, previous sentence translations, and pseudo-glosses are fed to a fine-tuned LLM; trains/evaluates on BOBSL and applies to How2Sign. It occupies contextual-cue fusion for sign-to-spoken-text translation. https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Jang_Lost_in_Translation_Found_in_Context_Sign_Language_Translation_with_CVPR_2025_paper.html ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.09754 . Abstract/method summary inspected; full PDF details remain to verify before making a close-method comparison.
- **Sign2GPT (ICLR 2024)**: gloss-free SLT using pretrained vision/language models through lightweight adapters, pseudo-gloss pretraining, and PHOENIX14T/CSL-Daily evaluation. It already makes the CV-to-LLM bridge an explicit research mechanism, while emphasizing limited parallel data and long-video compute. https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/4ebba705ffdee81e0a638c99fe066ce2-Abstract-Conference.html ; full paper https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/4ebba705ffdee81e0a638c99fe066ce2-Paper-Conference.pdf . Abstract/task framing inspected.
- **FLa-LLM (LREC-COLING 2024)**: its authors report that directly introducing an LLM can cause it to dominate training and leave visual representation insufficiently learned; they factorize visual pretraining and LLM fine-tuning. This is a direct warning against evaluating only fluency or BLEU and assuming visual evidence drove the output. https://aclanthology.org/2024.lrec-main.620.pdf . Abstract and method summary inspected.
- **MMSLT (ICCV 2025)**: uses off-the-shelf multimodal LLM-generated sign descriptions combined with visual sign features; reports gloss-free translation on PHOENIX14T and CSL-Daily. This further narrows any generic claim that CV sign features plus an LLM are a new combination. https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/papers/Kim_Leveraging_the_Power_of_MLLMs_for_Gloss-Free_Sign_Language_Translation_ICCV_2025_paper.pdf . Primary paper result/table snippet inspected, full method not yet fully audited.
- **SignEdgeLVM (Discover Computing, 2025; DOI 10.1007/s10791-025-09509-1)**: proposes shared relative-attention structure and dynamic point-frame sampling; evaluates on PHOENIX14T. Despite “edge” framing, its reported edge device is an i7-9750H laptop with 8 GB RAM, not a Raspberry Pi/microcontroller. Its split has signer overlap; its conclusion says energy was not measured. It reports teacher/student BLEU-4 22.68/22.55, but continuous gloss recognition/coarticulation remain difficult. https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10791-025-09509-1 , especially §§5.1–5.3 and §6. This means another Pi port or speed/size comparison is not a distinct thesis by itself.
- **SignGPT (arXiv:2609.21709, 2026-09-18)**: a very recent preprint proposes pose-based gloss-free sign translation and generation and an exploratory one-turn sign-to-sign response pipeline, evaluated on How2Sign (ASL) and PHOENIX14T (DGS), including 12 Deaf ASL signers for the exploratory interaction. This makes broad “LLM-mediated sign interaction” even less open; its preprint status and exact results need auditing. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21709 . Abstract and reference/section listing inspected.

## Top two formalizations

### MSL-VAR: variant-aware isolated lexical retrieval

- **[D]** Let `V` be a consented query signing clip; `B={(b_i, y_i, v_i, s_i)}` be licensed bank/demo clips with gloss `y_i`, variant metadata `v_i`, and performer group/age metadata `s_i`; `C(V,B)` is the CV model's visual top-k candidate set. The system returns a candidate or `unknown`; if a language model is used, it can reorder `C` but cannot add labels.
- **[A]** Each test signer is excluded from all training/reference clips; variant labels and meaning-equivalence are reviewed by MSL signers/linguists; public tutorial videos may not be scraped or redistributed without permission.
- **[T/C]** On an independent signer-held-out sample of licensed MSL lexical variants, context/variant-aware constrained ranking improves recall at a pre-registered false-accept ceiling over visual-only nearest exemplar and classifier baselines, without increasing unsupported labels. This remains a conjecture; the sample size and achievable bound are unknown.
- **Observable outcome:** signer-level recall@k, false-accept rate, coverage/risk curves, confusion within versus across same-meaning variants, latency/memory/energy on a chosen edge device.
- **Counterexample:** context prior ranks a plausible but visually absent label; apparent improvement disappears on held-out signer; age/variant metadata leakage identifies the source performer.
- **Negative value:** can show the public lexicon is suitable only for lookup/teaching and not CV training or that variance dominates models, blocking an unjustified translation product.

### MSL-CTX: visually grounded context assistance for bounded service phrases

- **[D]** For a video `V_t`, earlier conversational context `H_{<t}`, and allowed lexicon `L`, system emits MSL-to-target-text `z_t` or `ask/unknown`. The visual model proposes candidate signs `C(V_t)`; context is allowed to reorder candidates or assemble a human-reviewed phrase mapping, not invent signs.
- **[A]** The chosen target is explicitly defined (colloquial Cantonese text is not interchangeable with written Chinese); phrases are created with Deaf MSL participants and interpreters; train/test separate signer, session, and phrase templates.
- **[T/C]** At fixed semantic false-translation ceiling, a candidate-restricted context model improves exact/semantic phrase recovery or reduces confirmation burden over vision-only, context-only, SCOPE-style multimodal context, and always-confirm baselines. Otherwise report context-induced hallucination as the result.
- **Observable outcome:** semantic error reviewed by MSL experts, unsupported concept rate under context-swaps, risk-coverage, confirmations per correctly understood phrase, and edge end-to-end latency.
- **Counterexample:** hold context fixed and swap the critical sign; if output remains context-consistent rather than video-consistent, the mechanism is unsafe. Conversely swap context while holding video fixed to expose LLM prior dominance.
- **Negative value:** directly quantifies when linguistic priors help versus overwrite visual evidence in a low-resource local language; still requires a nontrivial, ethical dataset and strong direct baselines.

## Decision

**HOLD.** The most defensible lead is `MSL-VAR`, narrowed to licensed, isolated MSL lexical-variant retrieval with signer-held-out evaluation and an LLM restricted to visual candidates. It is not yet a research direction until a Deaf-led data-access conversation confirms reusable clips/labels, consent, and independent participants. A continuous “translate Macau sign language to Cantonese” system is not supported by the described public vocabulary-bank task and is crowded by SCOPE/Sign2GPT/contextual SLT. A Raspberry Pi demo is not the contribution.

First gate: confirm whether the user means spoken Macao Cantonese or Macau Sign Language. If MSL, get official bank rights/data format, select a narrow user task (learning lookup versus live service communication), and identify community/linguistic collaborators. If spoken Cantonese, reformulate around microphone/audio and do a new ASR/LLM search rather than reusing this sign-language packet.
