# Divergence Packet: 2026-09-03 weak-network data-plane and offline semantics

## Decision investigated

Whether a one-year, non-personal Raspberry Pi 5B edge FYP for a factory room, temporary work site, or mobile-repair setting can be substantively different from `START-WIT` by changing the observation model and action endpoint. `START-WIT` observes short current/accelerometer traces after a command and records a physical command outcome. This packet instead investigates communication scheduling, event-time semantics, offline authorization, sensor-record semantic compatibility, and privacy-preserving aggregation. At least one candidate is deliberately framed as a negative identifiability result.

## Top two formalizations

### WATERMARK-CLOSE

- **[D]** A source emits timestamped records with sequence number, source epoch, clock-error interval, and bounded or unbounded delivery delay. The hidden state is whether a record belonging to a declared window exists but is delayed.
- **[A]** At local deadline `T`, emit `complete`, `late-but-usable`, or `unknown`; optionally request a later review. No physical actuation and no remote decision is assumed.
- **[T]** The declared delay/clock model, local storage budget, and deadline are fixed before testing.
- **Observable outcome:** false-complete rate, unknown rate, closure latency, recovery after reconnection, and bytes retained/transmitted.
- **Counterexample:** two worlds produce the same sequence, clock, telemetry, and watermark before `T`; in one world an omitted event arrives after `T`, and in the other it never existed. Any policy restricted to the pre-`T` observations must act identically.
- **Negative-result value:** it would convert a vague “offline completeness” claim into a concrete boundary and identify the minimum added witness: source-side durable ACK, bounded-delay contract, or later synchronization.

### SCHEMA-QUARANTINE

- **[D]** The data-generating state is either semantically compatible or incompatible across a firmware/schema transition. Compatibility includes units, calibration meaning, aggregation operator, and provenance, not only parseability.
- **[A]** The edge node chooses `accept`, `convert`, or `quarantine` before synchronization; conversion is permitted only for declared reversible unit mappings.
- **[T]** CPU time, retained bytes, and local decision deadline are fixed; hidden semantic changes are held out from the guard design.
- **Observable outcome:** semantic mis-merge rate, unnecessary quarantine rate, conversion error, latency, and recovery after registry reconnection.
- **Counterexample:** firmware changes from RMS to peak while preserving schema ID, units, range, timestamps, and the observed finite trace. Without an independent fixture or signed semantic-version change, the two worlds are observationally identical.
- **Negative-result value:** a null establishes that local metadata cannot certify semantic comparability and prevents overclaiming that typed schemas provide numerical truth. A positive result must show incremental value over standard schema resolution and unit conversion.

## Decision

`WATERMARK-CLOSE` has the strongest negative-result value and should be tested first only if the team can predeclare a delay/clock model. `SCHEMA-QUARANTINE` is the best feasible alternative for a small Pi bench, but it is conditional on beating standard schema/unit checks and on staging a genuinely hidden semantic change. `CONTACT-UTILITY` is an edge-relevant systems alternative with high collision risk. `OFFLINE-AUTH` and `LDP-SUMMARY` are useful kill paths, not current thesis recommendations.
