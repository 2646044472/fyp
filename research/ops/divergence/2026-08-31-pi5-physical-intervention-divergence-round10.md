# Divergence Packet: 2026-08-31 Pi 5 physical-intervention and independent-witness directions

## Decision investigated

This round asks whether a Raspberry Pi 5B with an RGB Camera Module 3, a NoIR camera and an IR illuminator can support a reliability/sensing FYP whose claim is about a physical observation or intervention, rather than about deploying a new model. The output deliberately reopens the application story. It does not assume that lens contamination, RGB/NoIR fusion, or adaptive capture is novel.

## Top two formalizations

### PRF-TR: physical replay fidelity

- **[D] Decision:** For corruption family (d), a finite-budget maintainer chooses retain, reacquire, or unknown using a fixed decoder and a preregistered threshold.
- **[A] Action:** The action is evaluated under paired digital corruption and a matched physical intervention, with equal frame/energy budget.
- **[T] Target:** The target is exact marker validity (Y), supplied by the marker decoder plus a separately recorded clean-reference capture; physical cause labels are fixture-controlled.
- **Observable outcome:** Per-cell false-retain, false-reject, unknown rate, joules/frame, latency, and rank correlation between digital and physical policy outcomes.
- **Counterexample:** Two digital corruptions can preserve decoder confidence while a physical cover or cable fault changes color/IR response; alternatively, a brightness/blur threshold can rank all cells correctly. Either outcome is publishable as a boundary/negative benchmark, but not a method claim.
- **Negative-result value:** It tests a common hidden assumption in recent synthetic-fault benchmarks: that offline corruption severity is a valid proxy for a physical sensing failure.

### IR-CAUSE: counterfactual spectral witness

- **[D] Decision:** A local logger chooses keep, reacquire with IR, or unknown for a static marker.
- **[A] Action:** Capture RGB or NoIR once, optionally switch a safe IR illuminator and capture the second view; no adaptive model is required in the minimum experiment.
- **[T] Target:** (Cin\{\text{low visible light},\text{transparent cover/contamination}\}), independently assigned by lux-controlled fixture and film/cover state; (Y) is exact code validity.
- **Observable outcome:** Conditional cause-confusion, false-retain and joules per valid code under matched visible lux.
- **Counterexample:** A low-light scene plus a cover can produce the same RGB and NoIR observations (or auto-exposure can erase the intervention effect), proving that passive or IR-ratio cause attribution is not identifiable without an independent label.
- **Negative-result value:** The two-world indistinguishability result prevents an overclaim that a second modality “diagnoses” a physical cause.

## Queries and failed searches

- Queried physical camera sensor failure versus synthetic corruption benchmarks; found MSC-Bench, MapBench-style synthetic corruption, Gallon 2025 simulated camera faults, and Aher 2026 synthetic degradation supervision. No 2024--2026 paper was found that pairs Pi-class physical interventions with an independent scene oracle and tests a retain/reacquire action; this is a search result, not a novelty proof.
- Queried active illumination/value-of-information camera papers; results were dominated by adaptive exposure/ISP/modality selection and RGB-IR fusion. No inspected paper supplied the exact IR-on/off two-world cause-identification protocol; [GAP] pending validation citation-chain audit.
- Queried rolling-shutter flicker and timing; found RSSC 2026, Fukuda 2024, Otsuka 2024 and a VCIP 2025 flicker-removal record. The VCIP PDF and limitations were inaccessible, so FLICK-PHASE remains collision-prone.
- Queried camera power-supply transient plus image validity; no direct primary Pi-camera study was located. This candidate must not be promoted without a safe hardware protocol and a stronger literature search.
