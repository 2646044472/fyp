# Divergence Packet: 2026-08-31 physical-frequency and negative-observation sensing

## Decision investigated

Investigated whether a low-cost edge node can contribute a falsifiable sensing/reliability result through an independent physical challenge or an identifiability boundary, rather than through another RGB/NoIR modality selector, quality score, or deployment demo. The supplied Pi 5B, RGB Camera Module 3, NoIR camera, and IR illuminator are assumed available. No candidate is treated as novel; all are Amber until component, exact-claim, and boundary audits.

## Search boundary

Searches were run 2026-08-31 against arXiv, CVF Open Access, IEEE/ACM publisher records, official journal HTML, and official author repositories. Queries included:

- `camera lens contamination modulated illumination frequency response sensor diagnosis 2024 2025`
- `equal lux spectrum near infrared camera utility photopic illuminance 2024 2025`
- `active visual challenge camera integrity physical marker 2024 2025`
- `camera IMU motion blur action quality edge 2025`
- `event camera data loss illumination bias reliability benchmark 2025`

The field map was deliberately restricted to sensing, reliability, active acquisition, physical challenge/attestation, and metrology. Generic adaptive acquisition, sensor-health scores, fusion/routing, synchronisation, and passive anomaly detectors were treated as already occupied in the active state and were not regenerated as candidates.

## Recent-paper limitation map

1. **Optical contamination is measured, not physically identified.** The droplet study measures MTF and detector degradation after applying real droplets, but its evaluation is controlled illumination and post-capture analysis (Kim et al., *Effect of Droplet Contamination on Camera Lens Surfaces*, 2025, Secs. 2.1, 3.2, 4). Ma, Yan & Wu (arXiv v1, 2026, Secs. I-VIII, X-XIII, Suppl. XII) already provide passive clean-reference streaming health and camera-disjoint threshold transfer. This leaves only a narrowly stated possibility that a modulated IR response adds information before the task capture; it does not leave a generic contamination detector.
2. **Brightness is not a spectral witness.** Rampure et al. (arXiv:2606.17376v1, 2026-06-16, Sec. III-A and Sec. V) use a brightness threshold to choose RGB/NIR/thermal/low-light streams on heterogeneous robots. CIE S 017:2020 defines lux as photopic illuminance, so equal lux can hide a different spectral distribution; the Pi standard camera has an IR-cut path while NoIR omits it (Raspberry Pi Camera Module 3 product brief, p. 3). The unresolved question is an empirical boundary on whether a cheap spectral intervention changes an action ranking, not another brightness router.
3. **Active challenge and active fault diagnosis already exist.** Stamatakis et al. (IEEE Open Journal of the Communications Society 5, 1182-1196, 2024, pp. 1182-1183, Secs. I-II and pp. 1191-1193, Secs. V-VI) formulate semantic active probing as a POMDP with a threshold policy. Han et al. (arXiv v1, 2025-09-22, abstract and Sec. 1) select physical interventions for counterfactual perception fault diagnosis. Visual QR challenges for camera freshness were already demonstrated by Valente & Cárdenas (ACSAC 2015, pp. 141-150), and a 2025 patent explicitly challenges gain/preview parameters. Thus a physical challenge is only a possible *negative absence-observability test*; a general challenge-response method is a direct collision.
4. **IMU-assisted blur is a mature restoration/estimation family.** Arslan et al. (Sensors 23(16):7187, 2023, Secs. 1-3) use IMU data for blur-kernel estimation and note timing/noise limitations. Chen & Clark (ICCV 2025, pp. 90-99, Secs. 1-5) estimate camera velocity from one blurred image, while Wade et al. (SPIE 13468, 2025, abstract) compare inertial motion MTF with image MTF and report mismatch for multi-frequency vibration. A Pi+IMU implementation therefore cannot claim new deblurring or motion estimation; only an action-level finite attribution/negative benchmark remains.
5. **Event-camera robustness is itself being benchmarked.** Cazzato (Electronics 14(13):2603, 2025, Secs. 1, 4-8) releases controlled parametric data with two event cameras, moving targets, vibration, rotation, illumination and event-loss artifacts; the paper states that previous datasets were simulated or loosely controlled. Wu et al. (ICCVW MIPI 2025, Sec. 1, pp. 3865 onward) review hybrid-EVS deblurring under RGB+event capture. A new event-camera data-efficiency method is therefore occupied; a small physical-versus-digital event-loss ranking audit is the only defensible residual, and device cost is unresolved.

## Top two formalizations

### `IR-MOD-ID`

- **[D]** A finite cell is `(visible-lux band, cover state, contamination amount, lamp spectrum, camera path)`, with cover/contamination assigned by the experimenter before capture. The latent world `w` is not inferred from the image.
- **[A]** `RGB`, `NoIR+IR`, `PWM-IR(f1,f2)`, `fixed two-shot`, `scalar quality`, `always-reacquire`; each action has measured joules and delay.
- **[T]** A session is a fixed mount/target/lamp/cover cell. Train/freeze any thresholds on source cells; evaluate held-out cells and sessions.
- **Observable outcome** is exact-code success/failure plus `retain / unknown / reacquire`; use preregistered `10*false-retain + unknown + 0.1*normalized-joules` loss. The key statistic is held-out policy action-rank inversion with a session bootstrap confidence interval.
- **Counterexample**: choose clean low-light and cover worlds with the same RGB preview, lux, blur and metadata; if their PWM IR responses and exact-code risks also overlap, no policy based on these observations can identify the cause or safely select an action.
- **Negative-result value**: a rank-preserving null or two-world overlap states that adding IR modulation is not justified and supplies a finite physical boundary for future camera-health work.

### `LUX-AMBIG`

- **[D]** `D` contains a fixed printed code and three or more illumination spectra matched by photopic lux, plus held-out spectra/materials. The target action loss is predeclared.
- **[A]** `RGB-only`, `NoIR+IR`, `fixed two-shot`, `RGB/NoIR ratio challenge`, and `unknown`.
- **[T]** Hold exposure/white balance fixed and randomize capture order; leave lamp spectrum and specimen cell held out.
- **Observable outcome** is exact-code decode and the action-risk vector per spectrum, not lux itself. Test whether luma/lux-only policies and the spectral indicator induce different optimal actions.
- **Counterexample**: if all matched-lux spectra induce the same action ranking, the proposed spectral observation has no incremental value; if the spectrum is unmeasured, the apparent effect is confounded.
- **Negative-result value**: demonstrates either that lux-only commissioning is insufficient or that the additional sensor is unnecessary, preventing an unjustified adaptive-sensing thesis.

## Queries and failed searches

Failed/incomplete searches are explicit. No recent primary paper was found that tests a PWM IR frequency-response witness on a low-cost RGB/NoIR camera with an exact action-loss endpoint. No paper inspected proves that matched photopic lux necessarily changes a NoIR marker action ranking. The full quantitative pages of the MDPI event benchmark and MIPI challenge were rate-limited; these records are used only for component-collision evidence, not for a detailed novelty claim. No affordable event-camera/Pi driver and price were verified. The patent on camera parameter challenge-response was not treated as peer-reviewed evidence, but it is a practical collision risk.

## Decision

`IR-MOD-ID` is **HOLD (Amber)** only as a finite, active frequency-response value-of-information pilot; it becomes PIVOT if the modulation adds no held-out action value. `LUX-AMBIG` is **PIVOT (Amber)** to a negative identifiability benchmark unless matched-spectrum cells yield a repeatable rank inversion. `ABSENCE-PULSE` is **PIVOT/KILL** as a replication of visual challenge and semantic probing, retaining only the bounded “absence is not certified” counterexample. `IMU-BLUR-ATTR` is **PIVOT/KILL** as a finite action-level replication because blur/IMU estimation is occupied. `EVENT-DROP` is **KILL** as a low-cost thesis candidate and at most a literature-driven negative benchmark. No candidate is promoted; validation should first attack `IR-MOD-ID` with the fixed two-shot and scalar-ratio baselines.
