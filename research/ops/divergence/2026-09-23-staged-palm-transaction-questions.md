# Divergence Packet: 2026-09-23 staged Palm Payment transaction questions

## Decision investigated

Can the authorized **one staged Pi 5 Palm Payment FYP** support a falsifiable CS question beyond the December 2026 VIS live-ROI, open-set 1:N simulated-transaction MVP? Three strongest conditional questions: **T-RETRY, E-BOUND** for VIS falsification; **PA-CHARGE** only after the physical-attack gate. All HOLD. This packet does not change `research/active/`.

## Search boundary

[K] The 2026-09-13 meeting requires live capture, automatic ROI with failure/retry, identifier-free 1:N gallery search, a frozen unknown rule, simulated balance deduction and transaction history, local Pi operation, and stage/end-to-end timing. Every proposed evaluation ends in one logged `correct account debit / wrong account debit / unknown / retry / failure` outcome from the **physical hand presentation**, not an injected ROI. No real funds, production FAR, liveness or commercial-security claim. The MVP is a prerequisite, not an innovation. NIR follows repeatable vascular-information tests; physical attack/PAD follows a stable MVP and ethics/apparatus gate.

[KILL] Archive and active register already reject generic palm ROI/quality, Pi deployment, missing-modality routing, simple fusion, generated-ROI attacks and cross-device claims. Palm-ID already has mobile ROI, quality rejection, enrollment, open-set 1:N and latency; X-Palm even uses a Pi multispectral scanner. GBU-Palm already benchmarks physical print/replay RGB/NIR video PAD. Thus no hardware or new-model novelty is claimed. Bob Zhang's palm ROI, VIS/NIR and verification publications are thematic advisor fit **and** collision leads; the closest full papers still require validation reading.

Searched primary arXiv full HTML papers, NIST reports, ISO public scope and official author/institution listings on 2026-09-23. This is a bounded search, not proof of global novelty. A small volunteer cohort cannot estimate production-scale rare false debits; report person/session-clustered uncertainty and never infer a zero FAR from zero observed events.

These are three **different mechanisms**: sequential action, enrollment selection, and physical adversarial endpoint. All share complete capture/ROI/open-set 1:N transaction execution. An unknown-to-gallery volunteer is not a presentation attack. Separate unknown false identification from wrong match of an enrolled person. No candidate promises rare-event safety with 3-5 participants. NIR remains an authorized *roadmap upgrade*, not a research candidate in this handoff: stable vascular evidence and incremental value over fixed VIS+NIR fusion are unproved; archived P6 generic modality routing stays killed.

Pareto: T-RETRY is closest to December and has a measurable latency/denial consequence, but is near established retry methods and rare wrong debits need a much larger cohort. E-BOUND has a more exact longitudinal boundary but needs repeat visits. PA-CHARGE has the clearest adversarial harm but the strongest benchmark collisions and ethics burden. No aggregate score conceals those trade-offs.

## Top two formalizations

### T-RETRY

- **[D]** Episode `i`: supervised truth `Y_i in G union {unknown}`, ordered live captures `X_i1..X_iK`, ROI status, frozen matcher score vector and stage times. At step `t`, policy chooses `guided retry`, `unknown/fail`, or `commit(j)` exactly once. Wrong debit: `commit(j)` where `j != Y_i`; enrolled noncompletion: no correct debit by `K` or deadline.
- **[A]** Gallery, threshold, policy, nuisances and costs frozen before held-out people/sessions. Roster supplies truth, never the matcher. A prompt can alter the next acquisition distribution, so offline comparisons only use common supported actions; randomized/counterbalanced prospective prompts test the policy effect.
- **[T]** Compare person/session-clustered estimates of wrong-debit probability for unknown episodes, enrolled completion and p95 capture-to-debit time under identical opportunity/cost budgets. Predeclare practical effect and sample-size feasibility; this is not a statistical guarantee.
- **Observable outcome:** each ROI/search/action/debit trace, failure-to-acquire, first/last attempt errors, response to guidance, full latency and clustered intervals.
- **Counterexample:** conservative one-shot unknown always dominates, or current score/quality contains all predictive history; retries only multiply non-enrolled opportunities.
- **Negative-result value:** a bounded audit of transaction-level retry dependence and selection; if too few errors, report wide bounds/non-identifiability rather than “safe payment.”

### E-BOUND

- **[D]** Per supervised identity `u`, enrollment observations `E_u1..E_uk` have raw live ROI status, pose/session, quality and embeddings. Rule `h` selects at most `b` templates or refuses enrollment within minutes/bytes budget. Later mated and never-enrolled live palms traverse the same 1:N transaction path.
- **[A]** Account-person binding verified outside matcher; no evaluation session probe changes selection, gallery rule or threshold; equal capture opportunities and storage across policies. Failure-to-enroll and revisit costs remain in denominator. Session truth comes from acquisition logs.
- **[T]** Compare held-out vector `(wrong debit, enrolled noncompletion, failure-to-enroll, enrollment time, p95 transaction time)` at declared gallery size/unknown mix. Test whether cross-pose/session selection improves feasible frontier over best-of-k and same-session controls; no universal certificate.
- **Observable outcome:** enrolled/refused identities, later-session correct/unknown/wrong transactions and resource costs with person-clustered intervals.
- **Counterexample:** best-quality first-session image is as good as a costly revisit, or disagreement only reveals a matcher defect not actionable at enrollment.
- **Negative-result value:** directly quantifies same-session enrollment optimism in this protocol, without inventing an enrollment method.

## Decision

**HOLD.** The immediate December gate remains the VIS full transaction, not NIR, attack work or a paper claim. A well-scoped negative transaction audit is preferable to a renamed generic palm method.
