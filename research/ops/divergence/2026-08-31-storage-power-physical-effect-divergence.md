# Divergence Packet: 2026-08-31 Storage, Power, and Physical-Effect Evidence

## Decision investigated

Whether a low-cost, non-personal, non-production edge FYP remains possible after excluding the currently killed branches: biometric recognition, generic adaptive/virtual sensing, reference-action calibration, camera health, outage/log provenance, I2C recovery, ToF/vibration, generic redundant computation, OTA/lifecycle, and ordinary model deployment.  This pass deliberately examines three different mechanisms not represented as a live direction in the active register:

1. whether an edge logger has *device-local, empirically testable evidence* that a completed record survives sudden power loss on a removable card;
2. whether a hardware power-fail event can make a pre-failure sensor record admissible rather than merely report a reset; and
3. whether a commanded visible bench status has independently observed physical effect rather than only a GPIO/software acknowledgement.

No candidate is a novelty claim.  Every positive possibility is Amber pending the required component, exact-claim, and boundary/impossibility audits.

## Search boundary

Searches were run on 2026-08-31 and year-pinned to 2024--2026 where possible.  Primary papers, OS specifications, MCU documentation, and storage-vendor documentation were preferred.  Search terms covered `microSD sudden power failure`, `embedded flash crash consistency`, `fsync power failure`, `power failure warning NVM`, `brownout record validity`, `photodiode LED fault detection`, `edge physical actuation evidence`, and `sensor UART DMA loss detection`.

The pass does **not** propose a real service, a safety actuator, production firmware, personal data, or fault exploitation.  All proposed experiments are benign bench tests with synthetic records, deliberately controlled power interruption, and a reproducible post-reboot oracle.  A host return code, checksum, reset reason, or LED driver diagnostic is not assumed to establish a stronger physical fact than its documented scope.

## Top two formalizations

### FSD-WIT: empirical removable-media durability evidence

- **[D]** Fix a configuration `c=(host, card-model/batch, filesystem, log-layout, cut-waveform)`.  The logger emits records with strictly increasing sequence number and payload digest.  A cut offset `u` is selected from a preregistered interval relative to the commit operation.  After reboot an offline oracle observes recovered prefix `R`, filesystem mountability `M`, and checker output `o`.  The policy chooses either `retain` or `unknown`.
- **[A]** The oracle is never available to the policy at record time; cut offsets, cards, log layouts and checker thresholds are blocked before evaluation; payloads are synthetic; all storage media remain in the lab.  The claim is conditional on `c`, not on the SD specification or all cards sharing a label.
- **[T]** **Amber empirical target:** on held-out cut offsets for fixed `c`, a pre-registered journal/checker maintains a one-sided confidence bound on the event `policy=retain AND R is not the declared valid prefix`, while retaining more records than always-unknown.  A second held-out card checks whether the same bound transports; lack of transport is a planned negative result.
- **Observable outcome.** Raw power-cut timing from an offline reference instrument, rebooted filesystem image, recovered sequence prefix, card mountability, checker decision, write latency and joules.  The sequence prefix is independent of the policy because it was generated before the cut.
- **Counterexample.** Two card controllers can accept the same bus commands and return the same host API outcomes while one retains an interrupted FTL update and the other recovers it.  Therefore no policy based only on host outcomes can prove a universal card-agnostic durability statement.
- **Negative-result value.** A non-transporting bound is useful: it demonstrates that a low-cost logging study must name its storage configuration, and prevents an unjustified claim that a journal or `fsync` is physical evidence for arbitrary COTS media.

### POF-ADM: limits of power-fail evidence for record admissibility

- **[D]** Let `V(t)` be the unobserved supply trajectory.  The device sees comparator event `p` (0 or 1), reset reason `r`, record phase `q` (`acquire`, `serialize`, `commit`, or `idle`), and post-reboot checker `o`.  Ground truth `Y` is defined only for a listed benign cut experiment: the interval's payload and declared storage prefix recover according to the frozen protocol.  The local action is `keep` or `unknown`.
- **[A]** Comparator threshold, hysteresis, sleep state, interrupt routing and record phase markers are frozen.  The offline power trace labels `Y` but is not accessible to the local policy.  No claim concerns analog sensor correctness outside the declared cut cells.
- **[T]** **Boundary target:** compare a POF-aware action with `(reset reason, post-reboot checker)` under exactly matched cut cells.  A strict false-keep reduction is required at equal or lower unknown-rate; otherwise POF-ADM is killed and retained only as FSD-WIT instrumentation.
- **Observable outcome.** Comparator event, reset reason, phase marker, offline voltage trace, recovered record prefix, energy/time to restart, and policy action.
- **Counterexample.** A voltage excursion shorter than or outside comparator operation can leave `p=0` while disrupting an acquisition or commit; conversely a warning can arrive during an interval whose record already completed.  Thus the event cannot identify record truth without the post-reboot storage outcome.
- **Negative-result value.** A null result establishes that the board's brownout warning is a reset/ordering signal rather than an information-bearing record-quality witness.  That is valuable configuration evidence but not a new reliability method.

## Decision

**PIVOT.** Do not lock a thesis direction from this packet.

- **FSD-WIT: HOLD (Amber)** only as a tightly scoped empirical reliability-boundary investigation.  It is the sole candidate with an independent post-reboot outcome, a real technician decision, low-cost apparatus, and meaningful negative result.  Its central risk is that it becomes merely a card/filesystem benchmark, or that the standard journal-plus-sync baseline makes every policy distinction vanish.
- **POF-ADM: PIVOT** into FSD-WIT instrumentation only.  It does not supply an independent record-quality witness unless it demonstrably changes held-out false-retain loss beyond reset reason plus post-reboot recovery.
- **EFFECT-LED, UART-CONT, and CARD-CAP: KILL** as standalone FYPs.  They are useful fixture/preflight controls but their strongest simple baselines already implement the full claim scope.

No candidate has passed component collision, exact-claim collision, or boundary/impossibility audit.  FSD-WIT requires an adversarial direct-neighbor/citation-chain search before even a conditional promotion.
