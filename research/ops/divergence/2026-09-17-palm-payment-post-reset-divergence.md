# Divergence Packet: 2026-09-17 — Palm Payment post-reset research gates

## Decision investigated

The 2026-09-13 roadmap and Phase 1 plan answer the immediate scope question: **the Palm Payment Phase 1 MVP is engineering groundwork only**. It is a required, local, offline, payment-like 1:N demonstration; it is not itself an edge-computing contribution, biometric-security result, palm-vein result, PAD result, or production payment claim. [K] The plan explicitly freezes Fast-CC, local templates, one system threshold, unknown-user handling, retry on ROI failure, simulated money, deletion, and stage-level timing, while placing NIR, fusion, attacks, PAD and cross-device work outside Phase 1. [K] The minutes likewise say to work only on the Phase 1 VIS MVP and preserve interfaces for later modules.

The later research space is therefore conditional rather than empty. The strongest remaining positive hypothesis is a factor-controlled **action frontier** for ring/environment robustness (`RING-CELL` / `PALM-ROBUST-ACT`). A low-cost NoIR+controlled-NIR incremental-information study (`IR-INCREMENT`) remains possible only after an optical truth gate. A bounded guided-retry HCI study is feasible but not automatically novel. Physical attack and PAD work can return only after the MVP and a full physical capture protocol exist; the newest GBU-Palm benchmark makes generic RGB/NIR PAD or fusion claims especially crowded. If all positive gates fail, a preregistered negative result is the defensible research endpoint.

## Top two formalizations

### 1. `RING-CELL` / `PALM-ROBUST-ACT`

- **[D] Decision:** after one VIS capture, choose `match now`, one guided recapture, or `unable-to-decide`.
- **[A] Assumptions:** consented participants; ring state/type, pose, light and background assigned or logged outside the policy; identity, hand, session and capture order recorded; development threshold and rules frozen; no PAD or payment-security claim.
- **[T] Target:** at fixed development FMR and one-recapture budget, compare FNMR plus failure-to-acquire, attempts and p95 transaction time across held-out ring-by-environment cells against the transparent matrix.
- **Observable outcome:** independent condition label, ROI/landmark validity, matcher score, action, mated/non-mated truth, retry result and stage timings, aggregated with identity/session as the independence unit.
- **Counterexample:** the ring lies outside the effective ROI; the effect is entirely a detector/guide failure; ordinary augmentation or fixed recapture has the same frontier; or ring state is confounded with identity/session.
- **Negative-result value:** identifies whether “rings” are a real factor in this apparatus and where the failure occurs. A stable null protects the demo from ring-specific model work.

### 2. `IR-INCREMENT` / `IR-MOD-ID`

- **[D] Decision:** after an ambiguous VIS capture, choose VIS decision, one VIS recapture, one IR observation, or `unable-to-decide` under a fixed attempt/time/energy budget.
- **[A] Assumptions:** same participant/hand/transaction; exact camera and illuminator state logged; visible rejection and NIR geometry are documented; capture order/session are recorded; unimodal and simple fusion baselines are frozen; no palm-vein or liveness claim before validation.
- **[T] Target:** determine whether IR adds held-out decision value beyond VIS recapture and always-two-shot at matched cost. Primary outcomes are FMR/FNMR or attack-free mated/non-mated error as applicable, failure-to-acquire, retries, p95 time and measured joules.
- **Observable outcome:** paired frames/scores, fixed repeatability statistic, action, truth, timing and energy; the policy never sees the assigned physical cause label.
- **Counterexample:** IR is only a brighter or differently exposed VIS image; fixed two-shot or equal-score fusion is Pareto-optimal; apparent benefit vanishes under identity/session holdout; or matched low-light and transparent-cover worlds remain observationally indistinguishable.
- **Negative-result value:** establishes that the affordable path is not a palm-vein modality, or that it is only illumination assistance. This prevents a visually persuasive but unsupported fusion claim.

## Decision

**HOLD the thesis slot; proceed with Phase 1 as engineering groundwork.** The immediate work is the portable VIS 1:N MVP, its frozen decision rule, explicit unknown/retry paths, deletion, timing logs and physical Pi acceptance. Later candidates remain conditional in this order: `RING-CELL` / `PALM-ROBUST-ACT` as the strongest bounded positive lead; `IR-INCREMENT` only after the optical truth gate; `GUIDED-RETRY` as a bounded HCI alternative; and physical attack/PAD only as a post-MVP, full-capture, costed evaluation. `NULL-AUDIT` is the fallback research product if transparent controls dominate.
