# Divergence Packet: 2026-08-29 adaptive sensing and physical-query boundaries

## Decision investigated

Whether recent adaptive sensing work leaves a low-cost, non-personal-data edge direction whose contribution is a falsifiable **measurement action** rather than a new classifier, sensor fusion stack, or energy-saving sampling-rate rule.  I read `AGENTS.md`, the active README and charter, the candidate register, and the divergence instructions before searching.  This packet deliberately does not revive the killed CO2 calibration-age, camera-count, generic wake-up, ToF self-test, or selective-audit candidates.

The result is **not a promotion**.  Two candidate actions remain only as Amber HOLDs: (1) wake a physical environmental sensor or return `unknown` when a virtual reading is outside a predeclared calibration-support condition, and (2) choose a controlled pair of vibration test speeds to distinguish a mechanical trend from an attachment-dependent measurement change.  Both need an exact-claim audit.  Two apparently nearby directions are killed by directly inspected 2024--2026 primary papers.

## Recent-paper limitation map

1. [K] **Runtime virtual sensing is already an edge action, not an untouched gap.** Attarha and Forster (WONS 2026) intentionally deactivate sensors, reconstruct their readings from recent target history plus correlated companions, use a TinyML edge deployment, and calibrate a maximum dormant period.  Their smart-building case uses an ESP32, SCD30 CO2, and DHT11; their reported calibration horizon is seven days.  A proposal that merely predicts readings while a sensor sleeps is therefore occupied.

2. [K] **Generic low-power time-series fault detection is also occupied.** AssureSense explicitly detects subtle sensor faults on low-power edge devices from temporal, local, and cross-sensor features, and describes deactivating a malfunctioning sensor after detection.  It also documents the central ambiguity: faulty readings may remain in a normal numeric range while normal variability can look irregular.  A feature extractor or fault score cannot be the contribution here.

3. [K] **Adaptive stopping of vibration acquisition is already a measurement-constrained diagnosis task.** Chen et al. formulate `STOP/CONTINUE` on successive vibration windows, jointly optimize class prediction and acquired-window cost, and compare fixed-window and confidence-threshold controls.  Changing the model, SBC, or dataset would not differentiate a proposed early-stop direction.

4. [K] **Moving a camera for uncertainty-aware recognition is already active sensing.** Fan et al. model active recognition as sequential evidence gathering, let an agent move/turn/tilt to obtain new views, and include uncertainty from missing/poor views.  Combined with the active register's killed Q1 camera-count candidate, this prevents treating a servo camera plus abstention as a new direction.

5. [C]/[GAP] **There may be a narrower intervention boundary.** The inspected virtual-sensing paper chooses a calibrated maximum dormancy horizon, not an online `wake / virtual-output / unknown` decision driven by a frozen support test.  The inspected vibration papers use speed as an input or decide when to stop existing windows; none of the retrieved primary sources was an active, small-bench speed-pair design aimed at separating a physical-condition trend from sensor attachment change.  This is an incomplete source chain, not evidence of novelty.

## Top two formalizations

### AVS: support-gated physical wake

- **[D] State and action:** Let `x_t` be the observable companion readings, `h_t` the latest physical target readings, `d_t` time since a physical read, and `b_t` remaining physical-read/energy budget.  `pi(x_t,h_t,d_t,b_t)` chooses `virtual`, `wake`, or `unknown`.  An independently logged reference `y_t` is withheld from the online policy.
- **[A] Support condition:** The predicate `g(x_t,h_t,d_t)` is frozen from calibration data before the held-out perturbation block.  It may not inspect `y_t` or choose thresholds after seeing target labels.  A physical target reading obtained by `wake` is usable only after a preregistered warm-up rule.
- **[T] Target empirical claim:** At matched physical-read energy and a declared `unknown` budget, support-gated wake has lower `Pr(|reported_t-y_t| > epsilon AND action_t != unknown)` than fixed dormant duration, periodic wake, always-on, and unknown-only.
- **Counterexample:** Two held-out worlds have the same `(x_t,h_t,d_t)` but different target values `y_t` because a companion-target relationship breaks.  Any virtual/unknown decision that sees only those observations must act identically; only a wake or an added observation can separate them.
- **Negative-result value:** If fixed dormancy dominates, it rules out the claimed need for online support gating under this low-cost modality.  If the predicate has no transfer, it produces a bounded failure region for virtual sensing rather than a misleading energy claim.

### VSB: controlled speed-pair vibration query

- **[D] State and action:** From a first vibration window `z_0` at a safe initial speed, an edge policy may request exactly two `tau`-second windows at speeds `(r_1,r_2)` from a frozen finite set, then outputs `trend`, `measurement-uncertain`, or `inspect`.
- **[A] Ground truth:** The experiment independently records motor condition (fixed/added eccentric mass) and mount condition (specified clamp torque/fixture state); each target block holds out at least one joint load/mount cell.  The policy cannot inspect the held-out labels while choosing speeds.
- **[T] Target empirical claim:** At a two-window/byte/deadline budget, an ambiguity-conditioned pair lowers false `trend` records at no higher missed-trend plus `unknown` cost than fixed-one-speed, fixed-pair, and passive-long-window baselines.
- **Counterexample:** If a changed mount and an added eccentric mass generate indistinguishable two-speed features, no policy in this observation model can distinguish them.  The appropriate output is `measurement-uncertain`, not an unqualified diagnosis.
- **Negative-result value:** A condition map of indistinguishable mount/load cells or a result that fixed-pair dominates is still a credible, reproducible measurement-limit result.

## Queries and failed searches

- Searches for `active vibration speed selection measurement control` retrieved speed-as-input diagnosis, adaptive stopping, and passive feature work, but no inspected primary paper with VSB's exact speed-pair/separate-attachment endpoint.  This is a retrieval gap, not a positive novelty finding.
- Searches for `virtual sensing adaptive wake` retrieved WONS 2026 and a 2026 IAQ scheduling lead.  The latter's official publisher page returned HTTP 429, so no claim rests on it.
- Broad adaptive-sampling results overwhelmingly optimize energy, bandwidth, or classification accuracy.  Those mechanisms were excluded because the active state already contains direct collisions (AS1/E, Q1, C1, D1/D2).

## Decision

**HOLD.** Do not lock a direction from this packet.  AVS and VSB are the only Amber residuals worth an independent validation round, because each has a distinct physical action, a concrete hidden-label evaluation, and a useful negative boundary.  AVS has the strongest immediate direct-neighbor risk (WONS 2026 plus the uninspected 2026 IAQ scheduler); VSB has the largest exact-search gap and must be attacked before any feasibility build.  VSTOP and VIEW are **KILL**; REF is **PIVOT/HOLD** only as a stricter alternative if AVS is killed.
