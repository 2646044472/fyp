## Top two formalizations

### `PVP`: proxy-validity boundary

- **[D]** At time `t`, online information is `I_t=(x_t, logits_t, adaptation telemetry_t, resource_t, p_t)`, where `p_t` is a light/temperature measurement not computed from the camera image. Action is `{hold, adapt, accept, rollback, unknown}`.
- **[A]** Target labels are withheld online. `p_t` is selected before target evaluation and its valid region is fixed from source data or an independent calibration protocol. The proxy is not assumed to be causal.
- **[T]** On blocked physical-shift cells, a proxy-gated rule lowers `P(accepted update increases task risk by > delta)` at matched compute and abstention cost against no-adapt, always-adapt, fixed-reset, disagreement, and shift-trigger controls.
- **Observable outcome:** offline task risk, harmful accepted-update rate, abstention burden, recovery delay, CPU time, memory and energy.
- **Counterexample:** hold `(x_t, logits_t, telemetry_t, p_t)` fixed while swapping hidden labels so that adaptation is beneficial in one world and harmful in the other. Any honest policy must abstain or acquire a new trusted label/physical invariant.
- **Negative value:** if the proxy is redundant or fixed-reset wins, the result bounds when external ambient measurements do not justify label-free edge adaptation.

### `EBR`: electrical-bus action-identifiability boundary

- **[D]** Let `z` be a finite injected fault state (stuck SDA, incomplete address, unplugged target, transient NACK), `x` the pre-action wire trace/ACK history, and `a` in `{retry, clock-recover, power-cycle, manual}`.
- **[A]** Faults are injected only on a non-production bench; a logic analyzer or GPIO trace is ground truth for electrical state; the policy sees only the declared low-cost trace prefix.
- **[T]** A policy may choose a cause-specific recovery action only when two candidate states have empirically different post-action success distributions under the chosen action. Otherwise it must return `manual/unknown`.
- **Observable outcome:** successful read within deadline, recovery latency, bytes lost, register-side effects, and energy of recovery.
- **Counterexample:** an unplugged sensor and a permanently held SDA line can expose the same initial `SDA=0, SCL=1, NACK` prefix while requiring different optimal actions; no receiver-only policy can guarantee both.
- **Negative value:** a finite map of indistinguishable prefixes and standard-recovery outcomes is publishable as a boundary/replication result even when no new recovery algorithm wins.

- The exact empirical I2C action-identifiability endpoint was not retrieved. Standard bus-recovery documentation and partial-observation/cost-aware diagnosis work are mechanism collisions; a boundary result requires fresh experiments.

## Decision

`PVP` is **KILL** as a proposed direction.  Lens and BoTTA now occupy model-specific input quality, edge TTA, resource cost, and acceptance/evaluation endpoints; an external lux/temperature proxy is only an evaluation control or a negative boundary.

`EBR` is **PIVOT / Amber** as a non-affirmative electrical action-identifiability study.  Linux owns the recovery mechanism and recent literature owns generic partial-observation/cost-aware diagnosis; the only credible contribution is a finite, reproducible map of when the available I2C wire evidence cannot justify a cause-specific action.

`BRS` and `TAC` are **KILL** as proposed mechanisms because recent primary work already performs the same rescan/active-touch decision.  `CAM` is **PIVOT only** to a negative transfer/ambiguity boundary and should not be promoted as a detector.

Overall decision: **PIVOT**.  The next validation gate should test `PVP` and `EBR` against the strongest simple baselines and a two-world indistinguishability construction.  No direction is promoted by this divergence packet.
