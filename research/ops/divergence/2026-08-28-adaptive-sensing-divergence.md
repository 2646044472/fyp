# Divergence Packet: 2026-08-28 adaptive sensing and selective observation

## Decision investigated

Whether an adaptive-sensing / active-perception direction can replace or complement the locked C3 reliability direction while remaining a one-year, self-purchasable-hardware FYP: runnable benchtop demo by 2026-12 and experimental expansion in 2027-H1.

This is a divergence packet, not a claim that a candidate is new. The current C3 question is retained verbatim as a useful comparator: **“已有方法在某些设备上有效，换到一个没有参与调参的新设备、新负载或新故障条件后，还有效吗？”**

## Recent-paper limitation map

1. **Dynamic sampling itself is directly occupied.** iAirGuard (COMSNETS SysAI 2026) combines ESP32-S3 environmental sensing, variance/mean-driven dynamic sampling, TinyML AQI prediction, and a secondary fault score. Its claimed evaluation compares one continuous node against one adaptive node, and reports storage/transmission reduction. It explicitly acknowledges single-node testing and environmental interference (p. 3). A proposal framed as “low-cost adaptive air-quality sensing with drift detection” is therefore a component collision. [K]

2. **Uncertainty-triggered expensive measurement is directly occupied.** Han et al. (IEEE TSM 2025) adaptively choose expensive physical metrology from model uncertainty, benchmark fixed/random sampling and identify drift/shift and sparse initial data as the difficulty. It also shows a failure mode: as threshold approaches 1, insufficient samples cause model degeneration (p. 7). A proposal that merely trains an uncertainty model and samples more when uncertain is a direct-neighbor collision. [K]

3. **A gap worth testing, not asserting:** iAirGuard's reported comparison is a rate/data-volume comparison; its fault score compares sensor AQI to a local model prediction (pp. 4-5). In the inspected text, no independent always-recording reference trace, pre-registered event-duration label, matched-cost periodic/random audit baseline, or `safe / event / unknown` decision endpoint was found. This is an absence in two inspected papers only, not evidence that the gap is globally open. [GAP]

4. **Identifiability boundary.** If a policy observes only a cheap sentinel and does not acquire an independent verifier during interval `I`, then a world in which the target stays safe and a world in which the target crosses and returns within `I` can have the same policy-visible trace. The policy cannot truthfully distinguish them. This is a direct observation-model argument, not a claim of a new theorem. It means any adaptive policy must report `unknown`, impose a maximum gap, or retain independent audit observations before it can make a “no event” assertion. [K]

## Top two formalizations

### AS1: audited adaptive-sensing boundary

- **[D] Decision:** at time `t`, the local monitor chooses `a_t in {verify, skip}`; after an event window it reports `event`, `no-event`, or `unknown`.
- **[A] Action and budget:** `verify` activates/records the costly verifier; total verifier-on time and local energy/storage are fixed. A predeclared audit fraction `q` cannot be reassigned after seeing held-out data.
- **[T] Target:** a threshold excursion of duration at least `d_min` in a non-hazardous benchtop process. The original-time reference is a separately powered, continuously recording instrument; policy logs are not ground truth.
- **Observable outcome:** false `no-event` rate, missed-excursion duration, time to valid evidence, verifier duty/energy/storage, and unknown rate. Compare frontier to periodic, random, and change/uncertainty trigger at matched budget.
- **Counterexample:** a short excursion begins after a low-variation sentinel reading and ends before the next verifier observation. A self-only adaptive trace is compatible with both no event and that excursion.
- **Negative-result value:** if uniform periodic/random audit matches or beats adaptive triggers, it establishes that added local adaptation did not buy reliable evidence for the finite deployment family; the demo remains an honest benchmark.

### AS4: self-blindness under sensor drift

- **[D] Decision:** choose `increase rate / hold rate / request independent audit / state unknown` from sentinel history and health score.
- **[A] Action:** a finite family of threshold/change/uncertainty rules plus fixed periodic and random audit; no learned policy selected after target results.
- **[T] Target:** sentinel stuck-at, constant offset, slow drift, and external short-pulse process families, including a held-out joint cell.
- **Observable outcome:** false-clear / false-healthy rate at matched audit budget; time to fault recognition; maximum blind interval.
- **Counterexample:** the two trajectories `(true stable, healthy sentinel)` and `(true excursion, sentinel stuck)` yield the same sentinel trace until an independent measurement. Any deterministic self-only policy must make the same action.
- **Negative-result value:** an empirically reproduced blind region is a useful conditional reliability boundary; if it disappears under trivial periodic audit, the thesis collapses to a small engineering rule and should pivot to AS1's broader benchmark or C3.

## Queries and failed searches

- Searches listed under **Search boundary**.
- Searches returned broad active perception, edge scheduling, selective offloading, and multimodal fusion papers, but I did not accept them as primary evidence without inspecting their original text.
- The precise `independent reference trace + adaptive verifier audit + false no-event / unknown endpoint` combination did not yield an authoritative direct paper in these queries. This is a failed retrieval, not evidence of novelty.
- A candidate framed as `adaptive air-quality node`, `dynamic sampling with TinyML`, `sensor-drift detection`, `adaptive camera wake-up`, or `uncertainty sampling model` is already component-collided and should not be promoted without a different exact decision/evaluation boundary.

## Decision

**HOLD.** AS1 is the only fresh adaptive-sensing candidate worth a validation round, and only as an Amber, independently-labelled **reliability boundary experiment**, not an adaptive-sampling algorithm. AS2/AS4 may supply its semantic/impossibility framing but are likely to collapse to a maximum-gap or periodic-audit baseline. AS3 and AS6 collide with existing project pivots; AS5 lacks primary direct-neighbor evidence. The next gate is to actively try to kill AS1 with exact-neighbor and baseline searches before changing the locked C3 direction.
