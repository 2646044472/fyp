# Divergence Packet: 2026-08-31 sensing-systems divergence beyond IR intervention

## Decision investigated

Generate genuinely different Pi 5B + RGB Camera Module 3 + NoIR + IR-illuminator directions rather than extending IR-CAUSE, IR-MOD-ID, XFER-ACT, or POL-GLARE-VOI. I investigated resource-stress semantics, raw-versus-ISP observations, privacy/data minimisation, frame admission, and capture provenance. The packet deliberately includes likely kills and a negative-result path.

## Top two formalizations

### SLOW-CAP: stress-conditioned capture admission

- **[D]** A fixed printed target event is captured by RGB and/or NoIR; the Pi runs a named inference workload while stress (z_t\in\{none,cpu,mem,io\}) is injected. The observable stream is (o_t=(\hat y_t, q_t, \ell_t, cpu_t, mem_t, temp_t, queue_t)), where \(\hat y\) is decoder output, \(q\) decoder quality, \(\ell\) elapsed time, and telemetry is sampled locally.
- **[A]** Choose `retain`, `retry` (one extra capture), or `unknown`. The retry cost is measured in joules/latency; exact payload is the oracle.
- **[T]** Pre-register a static timeout, latency-EWMA, image-only, and telemetry-conditioned policy. Use held-out stress schedules and a fixed loss such as (10\,FR+1\,U+0.1\,J+0.01\,latency\).
- **Observable outcome:** false-retain rate, missed-deadline rate, unknown/retry rate, joules and latency; report action-risk curves rather than model accuracy only.
- **Counterexample:** Two runs can have identical image/decoder observations but different downstream queue deadlines; no image-only rule can identify the correct action. Conversely, if latency alone determines all failures, telemetry adds no contribution.
- **Negative-result value:** A rigorous null that static/latency-only policies dominate would show that resource telemetry is not worth the extra complexity for this Pi capture workload, and prevents a generic “adaptive edge reliability” claim.

### RAW-JPEG-GATE: representation-dependent reliability witness

- **[D]** For one physical exposure, retain a raw Bayer/DNG frame (r), processed JPEG (j), and capture metadata (m). The exact printed payload (y) is independently known. Candidate witness (w(r)) is a scalar linear-domain saturation/SNR/channel-occupancy statistic; baselines use (w(j)), blur, luma, and decoder confidence.
- **[A]** Choose `retain`, `reacquire`, or `unknown` before consulting (y). RAW and JPEG storage/processing costs are included in the budget.
- **[T]** Evaluate whether (w(r)) has higher conditional AUROC or lower cost at a fixed false-retain rate on held-out light/material/camera-path cells. Do not train a large restoration model.
- **Observable outcome:** exact-code failure risk, selective risk-coverage, bytes, latency, and joules.
- **Counterexample:** ISP metadata and JPEG luma may be a sufficient statistic for this marker; a raw statistic then cannot improve any action. Or raw capture overhead may dominate any saved recapture.
- **Negative-result value:** A matched-budget null would establish that the extra RAW path is unnecessary for a low-cost marker logger and constrain claims about raw-domain reliability witnesses.

## Queries and failed searches

The searches found no primary paper (within inspected 2024--2026 sources) that jointly evaluates Pi 5 RGB/NoIR raw-versus-JPEG statistics against an independently labelled exact-code retain/reacquire action. This is an unresolved search gap, not novelty evidence. Searches for `Pi 5 raw Bayer exact marker reliability`, `camera metadata mismatch QR edge`, and `telemetry-conditioned image decode timeout` returned product pages, issue reports, student projects, or generic timeout/ISP papers; no exact endpoint was verified.

The full ISM 2025 paper for exposure metadata verification was not available from the institution page, so its limitations and failure cases are unresolved. The UCC 2025 ACM full text was also inaccessible through the browser (403), although its official record exposes the abstract and section map; page-specific quantitative details should be rechecked from a downloadable copy before treating them as load-bearing.

## Decision

- **SLOW-CAP: HOLD (Amber), with a strong generic-timeout collision.** Only continue if a tiny preflight shows exact-code errors that cannot be explained by latency alone and a telemetry-conditioned action improves held-out stress cost.
- **RAW-JPEG-GATE: HOLD (Amber).** It is the most technically distinct observation endpoint in this packet, but raw capture support and a JPEG sufficiency baseline must be verified before any promotion.
- **EXIF-WIT: PIVOT / HOLD (Amber).** Treat as a bounded metadata-control audit only; full ISM limitations are unresolved.
- **FRAME-ADMIT: KILL as a thesis; keep as engineering baseline/negative result.** The Pi 5 poster already describes the mechanism and the fixed-period/motion baselines are strong.
- **PRIV-STORE: KILL as a thesis.** Pre-capture and on-device privacy mechanisms occupy the relevant endpoint, and real-person data are outside the project ethics scope.
- **CHAIN-REPLAY: KILL as a thesis; useful negative control.** Hash/watermark/provenance mechanisms are established and cannot certify scene truth.

Overall decision: **HOLD**. No candidate is promoted. The highest-value next gate is a two-hour SLOW-CAP/RAW-JPEG preflight on the purchased Pi: capture paired RAW/JPEG plus exact marker outcome while injecting one controlled CPU or memory stressor, then check whether either proposed witness changes the action-risk frontier against latency-only and JPEG-only controls. If both collapse, preserve the null result and return to the bounded IR pilot.
