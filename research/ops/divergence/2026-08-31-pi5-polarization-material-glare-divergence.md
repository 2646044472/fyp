# Divergence Packet: 2026-08-31 Pi 5 polarization, material-state and glare interventions

## Decision investigated

Investigated whether a Raspberry Pi 5B with the already-owned RGB and NoIR cameras, IR illuminator, and inexpensive add-ons (linear polarizer sheets, a rotatable filter mount, controlled lamp, lux meter, and optional force/water fixture) can support a defensible edge-sensing FYP direction in which a physical polarization/material intervention changes a concrete retain/reacquire/unknown decision. The packet deliberately does not defend the existing IR-CAUSE hypothesis: it tests optical-glare, moisture, birefringence/stress, and polarization-security alternatives against recent primary work.

## Recent-paper limitation map

* **Transparent-film/glare restoration is occupied.** Tang et al. (CVPR 2024, pp. 24987--24996; arXiv v1 2024-03-07, Secs. 1, 3.1--3.5, 4.1--4.3, 5--6) define wrinkled transparent-film removal, use four polarization angles, paired covered/uncovered industrial captures, and evaluate QR/OCR downstream. Their model assumes a fixed industrial posture/light pipeline and uses auto-exposure/auto-focus; their future conclusion is restoration, not an edge trust decision. This kills a new Pi film-removal network, but leaves a narrowly different question about whether a physical polarizer changes a frozen exact-code accept/reacquire action.
* **Large-scale reflection removal is occupied.** Yao et al. (CVPR 2025, pp. 10890--10899; arXiv v1 2025-03-23, Secs. 1--3, 4.1--4.2, 5) provide 6,500 aligned RGB/polarization pairs and a diffusion prior. They explicitly call reflection separation ill-posed and note dependence on multiple angles, scene variation, and lighting. A Pi restoration or RGB/polarization fusion claim collides; a matched-cost physical-intervention versus digital-corruption audit remains an empirical boundary only.
* **Polarizer orientation can be a physical intervention, but not a new mechanism by itself.** Venkatesulu et al. (Applied Optics 64, 1443--1453, published 2025-02-13; Secs. abstract and pp. 1443--1453) quantitatively measure contrast through water as a function of viewing angle with/without a vertical polarizer. Maximum contrast is near Brewster angle, while contrast-enhancement ratio continues increasing at larger angles. This supports a water/glare fixture and an angle-action endpoint, but not a universal optimal angle.
* **Glare mitigation already has an edge-reliability endpoint.** Khandaker et al., *How to deal with glare for improved perception of Autonomous Vehicles* (arXiv v1 2024-04-17, Secs. I--III, V, IX) compare object detection, recognition, tracking, depth and lane metrics. Their deconvolution requires a calibrated glare-spread function and enough dark pixels; saturation violates linearity, and the method assumes out-of-FoV glare can be handled by a hood. A polarizer experiment must therefore use exact task truth and compare the fixed filter, digital deglare, and no-filter controls.
* **Polarization-specific attack and defense are direct neighbors.** Li et al., CVPR 2024, pp. 24706--24715 (arXiv v1 2023-03-31, Sec. 6, pp. 24713--24714) show locally controllable polarizing projection can fool glass-segmentation and shape-from-polarization. They report low attack success in bright environments and suggest active illumination as a future protection. Zhang et al., *Laser Shield* (DAC 2024, six-page paper, Secs. 2.2--4, pp. 1--4) already combines a physical polarizer with min-energy angle rotation and evaluates defense success, functionality preservation, fault tolerance, and computation on LAPA. A new polarizer-rotation defense is therefore KILL unless the target, observation, and endpoint are substantially narrower and independently reproducible.
* **Material/transparent-object classification is occupied.** Yu et al. (Optics Communications 555, 130246, version of record 2024, Secs. 1--4) build an input-extensible edge-enhanced transparent-object segmentation network and a 496-image multi-polarization dataset. Li et al. (Scientific Reports 14, 2355, published 2024-01-29, Secs. Results/Discussion/Methods) classify six transparent plastics using birefringence-related DoLP/stack-standard-deviation features in a laser/QWP/holographic system; they require a calibrated 20-µm optical setup and explicitly note turbid natural water, more natural samples, and an objective lens as future work. A new material classifier or a Pi-only deployment is not defensible.
* **Photoelastic stress measurement is an established endpoint.** Efferz et al. (Glass Structures & Engineering 9, 439--454, published 2024-08-08, Secs. 1, 3--5) use phase shifting and multiple-wavelength photoelasticity to evaluate edge stress in tempered glass; the chamfer makes direct edge retardation unavailable and requires extrapolation. The 2024 Materials Science and Processing paper on in-line Si wafer stress imaging (DOI 10.1016/j.mssp.2024.108449) validates polarized stress images against finite-element models at high throughput. A quantitative stress meter is therefore a direct collision; only a bounded “does a cheap stress cue predict an exact-code failure?” endpoint remains Amber.
* **Wet/moisture sensing has nearby physical modalities.** Vassilev et al. (IEEE TITS 25, 12829--12834, DOI 10.1109/TITS.2024.3360702) distinguish wet and dry road surfaces using 77--81 GHz polarimetric radar in real traffic, with wet asphalt showing reduced entropy. Xu et al. (Transactions of the Chinese Society of Agricultural Engineering 40(3), 219--226, published 2024-02-15, DOI 10.11975/j.issn.1002-6819.202307204) use a focal-plane polarization camera for leaf moisture, reporting mean relative error 9.50% and RMSE 4.53%. These are not the same visible Pi task, but they kill a broad claim that polarization moisture sensing is new; a finite material/illumination invariance test is only Amber.
* **Polarization calibration and pixel/angle errors are nontrivial.** Fu et al. (PLOS ONE 19(1):e0296397, published 2024-01-30, Secs. 2--4) describe micro-polarizer interpolation, field-of-view alignment error, and limited spectral/working range. Ma et al. (Optics and Lasers in Engineering 184, 108589, version 2025) report a universal division-of-aperture calibration with subpixel alignment and DoP/AoP errors. A clip-on single polarizer on Pi RGB/NoIR cannot claim full Stokes quantities without calibration; all candidate protocols should use robust ratios/paired readings and record filter angle.

## Top two formalizations

### POL-GLARE-VOI

* **[D]** Let (s) be a fixed printed marker scene, (f\in\{0,1,\ldots\}) a physical glare/cover cell, (a\in\{\text{RGB},\text{fixed-polarizer},\text{rotate-once},\text{two-shot}\}) an allowed capture policy, and (Y_a) its Pi image(s). The independent outcome (O\) is exact payload equality from the marker generator, not model confidence.
* **[A]** Static target; rigid camera/target mount; manual or logged exposure/white balance; lux and capture energy recorded; polarizer transmission axis and angle measured; glare fixture labels independent of image output; no motion claim.
* **[T]** For a predeclared glare-cell distribution and equal expected capture budget (B), the rotate-once policy has lower false-retain (P(\hat O=\text{retain},O=0)) than every image-only baseline, or lowers false-retain at no greater latency/energy. [C] This is deliberately an empirical target; no effect size is assumed.
* **Observable outcome:** per-cell exact decode, false-retain, unknown/reacquire rate, latency, energy, lux, exposure/gain, angle and fixture state. A paired bootstrap over scene/fixture blocks gives uncertainty.
* **Counterexample:** Two scenes with the same preview brightness/blur but opposite polarizer benefit; or a digital deglare/always-two-shot policy with equal or lower risk. This forces KILL/PIVOT rather than tuning the angle post hoc.
* **Negative-result value:** a preregistered map of when a cheap polarizer does and does not change a real edge action, plus a physical-versus-digital glare transfer table.

### POL-WET-STATE

* **[D]** Let (m\in\{\text{dry},\text{wet}\}) be a physically applied surface state, (\ell) a held-out lamp/lux cell, and (r(Y)=\frac{I_{\perp}-I_{\parallel}}{I_{\perp}+I_{\parallel}+\epsilon}) a robust orthogonal-polarizer statistic over a fixed region. The independent label is delivered-water mass or a predeclared film protocol, not the camera image.
* **[A]** One surface family and finite water-film thickness range; no claim about arbitrary liquids, roughness, or safety friction; fixed geometry; intensity clipping excluded or separately reported; tile/sample split held out.
* **[T]** The worst-cell error of a threshold on (r(Y)) across held-out lux cells is lower than RGB mean/variance, saturation, blur, and always-on-lamp baselines at matched compute. [C] The physical mechanism is specular polarization from a film, but material and angle can erase the signal.
* **Observable outcome:** dry/wet error, calibration transfer across lamps and specimens, latency, energy, ratio variance, and mass/lux metadata.
* **Counterexample:** A matte rough tile or diffuse wetting layer depolarizes reflection so that (r(Y)) changes only with brightness; then the ratio cannot identify state. The correct output is a bounded null result.
* **Negative-result value:** a controlled illumination/material ambiguity map and an explicit statement that low-cost visible polarization cannot certify generic moisture.

## Queries and failed searches

* Searches for `adaptive polarizer angle edge camera 2024 2025` returned active-viewpoint, polarized reflection-removal, and Laser Shield neighbors, but no source establishing a Pi-class exact-code decision endpoint. This is a [GAP], not evidence of novelty.
* Searches for `visible polarization wet dry camera 2025` found radar wet-road and leaf-moisture studies, but no controlled Pi RGB/NoIR benchmark. Domain transfer is unverified.
* Searches for `polarization stress transparent cover edge camera 2024 2026` returned photoelastic metrology, stress-imaging instrumentation, and a 2026 phone-glass patent; no peer-reviewed Pi reliability-sentinel study was verified. The patent is not used as central evidence.
* Searches for `polarization attack defense phone screen 2025` found Li CVPR 2024 and Laser Shield DAC 2024 as the strongest direct neighbors; an adaptive-attacker result against a two-angle exact-marker detector remains [GAP].
* Paywalled or rate-limited full texts (Venkatesulu article curves; some ScienceDirect material papers) prevent verification of detailed numerical limitations. Only the official abstract/metadata facts above are treated as [K].

## Decision

**POL-GLARE-VOI: HOLD (Amber).** It has a real edge decision, an explicitly different action endpoint, and a small physical pilot, but Tang/PolarFree/glare papers make a restoration or “polarization improves images” claim unavailable. Promote only after a fixed-filter/two-shot/digital-deglare comparison shows a reproducible action-risk difference.

**POL-WET-STATE: HOLD (Amber).** Feasible and independently labelable, but the broad moisture claim collides with radar and leaf polarimetry. Keep the scope to one surface family and illumination-held-out binary state; kill if RGB or a fixed lamp matches.

**POL-STRESS-TRUST: PIVOT.** Photoelastic stress measurement is too established for a stress-estimation thesis. Retain only as a bounded failure-prediction sentinel tied to an exact-code oracle; otherwise use it as a physical-vs-digital negative cell.

**POL-SEC-ANGLE: KILL as a generic defense; PIVOT only for a benign detector-specific audit.** Li CVPR 2024 and Laser Shield DAC 2024 directly occupy polarization manipulation/rotation defense. Do not buy a laser or claim security unless a safe LCD fixture and adaptive-attacker threat model are independently available.

**POL-TRANSFER-REAL: PIVOT.** This is the most honest fallback: a finite paired physical/digital transfer benchmark with action-risk rankings. It has scientific value if null or inversion results are preregistered, but no positive thesis claim should be made before the pilot.

Overall handoff: **HOLD (Amber) for POL-GLARE-VOI and POL-WET-STATE; PIVOT for POL-STRESS-TRUST and POL-TRANSFER-REAL; KILL generic POL-SEC-ANGLE.**
