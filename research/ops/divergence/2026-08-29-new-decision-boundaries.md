## Recent-paper limitation map

1. **Label-free TTA acceptance is already a direct decision family.** AETTA estimates post-adaptation accuracy from prediction disagreement without target labels and demonstrates recovery; Hybrid-TTA detects shift from temporal input correlations and changes its tuning mode.  A 2025 NeurIPS paper additionally provides label-free risk monitoring with sequential tests.  These sources rule out a claim that an edge device's logits, disagreement, shift score, or a rollback action alone are a new acceptance controller. [K]

2. **The remaining TTA issue is an information boundary, not a ready mechanism.** Two worlds can share an unlabeled stream, logits, adaptation losses, checkpoints, and device telemetry while their hidden labels make opposite `accept` versus `rollback` actions correct.  An independently valid physical invariant or an exogenous delayed-label channel would have to break this ambiguity.  The archive has not identified either for the proposed lab setting. [C]/[GAP]

3. **Adaptive edge video retention is directly occupied.** TileClipper selects frame tiles at the edge to reduce uplink while preserving detector accuracy, reports Pi 4/Jetson Nano execution, and includes a live deployment.  A proposal to select local video regions or snippets under a byte/bandwidth budget would therefore need a different observation, action, and endpoint than data reduction plus detection accuracy. [K]

4. **Secure-camera provenance and recapture resistance have a hardware root of trust.** Scoop addresses recapture attacks against provenance-based media authentication with depth sensing/estimation, an iPhone/Android implementation, a 78-scenario dataset, and explicitly assumes a provenance-asserting secure camera.  A normal USB camera attached to a Pi cannot establish capture provenance merely by later hashing frames. [K]

5. **Wi-Fi sensing privacy is not a lightweight "add random interference" contribution.** Sharma et al. study conservative interference injection to reduce Wi-Fi CSI keystroke sensing while modelling bandwidth loss.  The article page was rate-limited before its methods/results could be inspected, so it is a direct-neighbor lead rather than evidence for any numerical comparison. [K] bibliographic; [GAP] exact protocol.

## Top two formalizations

### M1: proxy-validity boundary for on-device TTA

- **[D] Decision:** Given source model `f0`, recent unlabeled window `U_t`, current checkpoint `f_t`, prediction/logit/disagreement telemetry `q_t`, local resource state `r_t`, and an independently measured physical proxy `p_t`, choose `hold`, `adapt`, `accept`, `rollback`, or `unknown/inspect`.
- **[A] Assumptions:** labels are unavailable to the online policy; `p_t` is recorded without consulting labels and has a predeclared relation to an identifiable subset of physical shifts.  The relation must be tested source-to-target, not selected after target labels are examined.
- **[T] Target:** On held-out physical shift cells, a validity-gated policy lowers accepted harmful updates, `1[R(f_t)-R(f_ref)>delta]`, at a declared compute/abstention budget relative to no adaptation, always adaptation, fixed reset, prediction-disagreement acceptance, and a dynamic shift trigger.
- **Observable outcome:** harmful accepted-update rate, task loss after offline labels are revealed, abstention burden, recovery delay, CPU/GPU time, energy, and checkpoint memory.
- **Counterexample:** retain exactly the same image stream and all `q_t,r_t,p_t` values but swap the unobserved class/label mechanism so that an update improves risk in one world and worsens it in the other.  No unlabeled-only acceptance rule can choose different actions; any claimed certification must abstain or add an observation.
- **Negative-result value:** either shows that the physical proxy fails to delimit a valid label-free region, or that simple no-adapt/fixed-reset controls match it.  Both prevent a misleading edge-TTA deployment claim.

### V1: diagnostic evidence-retention boundary

- **[D] Decision:** At each episode the node observes video-derived object/fault telemetry, service/resource logs, and remaining bytes `b_t`; it chooses a compact evidence action: no raw retention, a short clip/tile subset, or an immutable reason-code/hash record.
- **[A] Assumptions:** a fault class is independently scripted and hidden from the retention policy; the later diagnosis task has a predeclared label rubric; reason codes cannot name the ground-truth fault.
- **[T] Target:** At equal retained bytes and no access to discarded media, a frozen selector improves blinded root-cause recovery on a held-out fault/scene pair relative to FIFO, retain-on-alert, fixed periodic snippets, and uniform random retention.
- **Observable outcome:** diagnosis accuracy by fault type, byte use, per-episode retention delay, retained-video exposure duration, and object-event recall.
- **Counterexample:** two faults produce the same retained log/clip under a selected policy but require different diagnoses.  No later analyst can distinguish them, so a retention score that calls either "diagnosable" is not valid.
- **Negative-result value:** a result that retain-on-alert/FIFO is sufficient is still an operational evidence result, but it kills V1 as a research mechanism.
