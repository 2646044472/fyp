# Round-29 GALLERY-CHURN Reconciliation (2026-09-24)

## Decision

`GALLERY-CHURN` is **KILL as a dynamic threshold, gallery-admission, recalibration or matcher method**. It reduces to the already rejected `G-ADMIT` family plus known gallery-size, open-set and template-aging effects. Retain only `GALLERY-CHURN-NULL`: a Palm-specific benchmark/negative result on whether fixed-gallery open-set measurements transfer to explicitly replayed gallery add/remove/update states.

## Exact surviving question

Under a frozen Palm matcher and threshold, do person- and time-blocked gallery states created by predeclared add/remove/update operations change FPIR/FNIR-like outcomes, rank/margin distributions or terminal unknown/action risk enough that a fixed-gallery benchmark is not a valid proxy? Compare fixed threshold, gallery-size stratification, max-score order-statistic controls, recalibration and refusal.

This is a finite protocol audit. Public Palm datasets do not supply real authorized account lifecycle events, so synthetic replay must be labelled as synthetic and cannot establish a live payment claim.

## Kill conditions

- fixed threshold plus gallery-size stratification explains the effect;
- recalibration or max-score order statistics dominate any proposed policy;
- add/remove/update sequences are not independently specified or person/time-blocked;
- no lawful lifecycle labels or terminal action outcomes exist;
- the result is only a renamed gallery calibration or matcher method.

## Project ordering

The preferred order remains: frozen VIS all-start trace; then `PALM-STOP-NULL` if continuous frames are available; then `E-BOUND-X` if three disjoint visits are feasible. `GALLERY-CHURN-NULL` is a lower-priority synthetic benchmark and should not trigger new data collection or hardware purchase.
