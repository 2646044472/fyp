# Direction Convergence Gate (2026-09-23)

## Decision

No thesis direction is promoted. The search has converged to one committed engineering route and two conditional research gates:

1. **Palm Phase 1 remains the execution route.** Complete the Raspberry Pi 5 visible-light physical capture -> live ROI -> identifier-free open-set 1:N -> frozen unknown rule -> idempotent mock debit flow. The first research-grade artifact is an all-start stage-count trace plus one complete physical transaction.
2. **VIS-GUIDE is a conditional evaluation gate.** After the payment executable is stable, test one prospectively assigned placement prompt or retake against the strongest fixed guide/quality-margin retake. The endpoint is all-start completion and capture-to-action time, not a new retry method or rare wrong-debit guarantee.
3. **AUDIT-GAP is a bounded non-Palm audit gate.** Do not frame it as adaptive sensing. It may proceed only as an independent-reference reliability audit with a benign physical event source, an independently powered and clocked reference, preregistered episode and `unknown` semantics, matched periodic/random/always-verify/abstain controls, and a held-out physical split.

`RAW-JPEG-GATE` and `IR-CAUSE` are not additional thesis routes. RAW is restricted to a capability or JPEG-sufficiency/null appendix; IR is restricted to a bounded active-IR action/identifiability pilot. Both require physical preflight evidence before implementation effort, and neither displaces the Palm route.

`P1` cross-device robustness versus cross-service linkability is likewise not a Palm Phase 1 or automatic later-phase thesis. Its positive method and monotone-tradeoff claim are killed; a finite audit is only a separate PIVOT after lawful crossed identities/devices/sessions, an actual two-service threat model and standard protected-template baselines are secured.

## Research claim boundary

The Raspberry Pi board, deployment, model choice, ROI implementation, NIR add-on, PAD component, adaptive sampler, or energy profile is not novelty by itself. Any promoted claim must specify the observation model, allowed action, resource constraint, direct baseline, independent endpoint and a negative-result condition. Search absence is not evidence of novelty; the current packets record only the inspected source boundary.
