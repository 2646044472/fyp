## Decision

The user-confirmed one-project route is the Pi 5 Palm Payment prototype. ## Exact question and boundary

[C] With a frozen gallery, matcher, threshold, capture opportunities and deadline, can attempt history (`ROI status, score, margin, elapsed time, prior outcome`) select `guided retry / unknown / commit once` to improve the **unknown-person wrong-account debit, enrolled-person correct completion and p95 full-transaction time** frontier over the strongest fixed 1/2/3-shot, Palm-ID-style quality retry, margin reject and conservative one-shot policies at matched opportunity and cost?

An unknown-to-gallery bona-fide person is not a presentation attack. No real debit, production FAR, liveness or PAD claim. The account/person roster provides independent truth; matcher score cannot label truth. Physical acquisition, ROI failure, identifier-free search, decision, idempotent mock debit and end-to-end latency are all in the denominator. No test subject or session may set threshold or policy.

## Direct-neighbor constraint

- [K] Palm-ID (arXiv:2401.08111v1, 2024-01-16, Secs. II-F/G, III-B/C/D and IV-A/B, https://arxiv.org/html/2401.08111) already studies mobile ROI, quality recapture and open-set 1:N. This kills deployment, quality retry or open-set metrics as novelty.
- [K] Czajka and Bowyer (BTAS 2015, DOI 10.1109/BTAS.2015.7358797, Secs. 1-5, author full text at https://www.researchgate.net/publication/279911940_Statistical_evaluation_of_up-to-three-attempt_iris_recognition) study correlated three-attempt biometric transactions. A dependence observation alone is not novel.
- [K] NIST IR 7544 (2008, Secs. 2.1.1-2.1.3 and 4-6, https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/IR/nistir7544.pdf) treats quality-guided enrollment/reacquisition and same-session optimism. A best-image or fixed retry control is mandatory.
- [GAP] The closest palm/face sequential-decision citation chains and Bob Zhang full papers have not been exhaustively audited. Exact residual novelty is Amber even if a local effect appears.

## Gate sequence

1. **MVP:** With documented consent/institutional permission, one enrolled and one unenrolled person physically present palms to the Pi. Automatic ROI, frozen open-set unknown rule, correct/unknown/retry outcomes, single idempotent mock debit, deletion and capture-to-decision log work end to end. Unit tests and prerecorded/cropped images do not pass this gate.
2. **Feasibility count:** Independently roster enrolled and never-enrolled participants; record people, sessions, acquisition failures, actual retry frequency and score distributions without fitting the proposed policy. Specify minimum practically meaningful frontier difference and uncertainty width before confirmatory collection. Repeated frames from one hand are not independent subjects. With zero errors in `n` independent unknown episodes, the rough 95% upper bound is `3/n`; a small pilot cannot assert rare-event security.
3. **Frozen comparison:** Pre-register training/held-out people and sessions, gallery size, threshold, K <= 3, deadline, guidance, cost/opportunity budget, baselines and stop rule. Offline replay compares only actions supported by the recorded capture sequence; randomized/counterbalanced live prompts are needed where guidance changes the next frame. Report separate unknown-person wrong debit, enrolled wrong-identity debit, correct completion, unknown/retry/failure, per-stage and p95 end-to-end time, with person/session-clustered intervals.
4. **Decision:** A repeatable, held-out non-dominated action-cost difference beyond every simple baseline earns a further exact-claim literature and advisor audit, not automatic PROMOTE. [KILL] Too few independent errors/people, no supported counterfactual, or baseline dominance closes the positive policy. A bounded dependence/selection/null report remains; do not tune a threshold after seeing held-out labels to manufacture errors.

## Later alternatives

`E-BOUND` requires initial enrollment, a distinct optional confirmation visit and an untouched later outcome visit; refusal and revisit costs count from the original eligible population. `PA-CHARGE` requires stable MVP, consent/ethics and a physical print/display attack apparatus, then lineage-held-out attack-to-ROI-to-1:N-to-mock-debit evaluation. GBU-Palm (arXiv:2608.14389v1, 2026-08-14, Secs. 2-4, https://arxiv.org/html/2608.14389) occupies generic physical RGB/NIR PAD. NIR cannot be called vein recognition until repeatable vascular evidence survives visible texture, crease and brightness controls.
