# Round-26 STAGE-RISK Reconciliation (2026-09-24)

## Decision

`STAGE-RISK` is **KILL as a positive thesis method**. Biometric performance standards, transaction-level reporting, reliability composition, selective prediction, calibration and ordinary stage logging already occupy its proposed components. The exact Pi 5 palm/mock-debit endpoint is a local evaluation opportunity, not evidence of a new method.

## Surviving boundary

Retain only `STAGE-NULL`, a bounded negative-result and identifiability audit:

1. Construct a two-stage counterexample showing that identical marginal stage rates can yield different joint terminal-action risk under different dependence structures.
2. Define the minimum all-start episode trace needed to identify the terminal outcome: capture opportunity, ROI reachability, matcher/open-set result, policy transition, action result and timing.
3. Compare product-of-marginals, direct episode frequency, stage-stratified logistic/isotonic calibration, matcher-confidence and fixed unknown/retake/refusal controls.
4. Test whether stage attribution remains unidentified without randomized or counterbalanced interventions.

This is not a new risk estimator, dashboard, logging schema or calibration algorithm. It is useful only if the trace exposes a reproducible gap between component summaries and the terminal action, or proves that the gap cannot be resolved under the available observations.

## Project decision

Continue the Palm Payment MVP first. Do not collect rare unknown or attack samples solely for `STAGE-RISK`; the candidate has no justification for new hardware, a larger model or a new biometric dataset before the basic all-start transaction trace exists.
