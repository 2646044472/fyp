# Round 20 Reconciliation: Null-Boundary Closure

## Decision investigated

Whether the post-`START-WIT` candidates `CAUSE-NULL` and
`OFFLINE-REV-NULL` can become the FYP direction.

## Evidence checked

- [Null-boundary divergence packet](../ops/divergence/2026-09-03-null-boundaries-divergence.md)
- [Null-boundary validation audit](../ops/validation/2026-09-03-null-boundaries-validation.md)
- Active candidate register and Round 19 reconciliation.

## Reconciled result

[KILL] `CAUSE-NULL` is not a primary direction. A one-channel
process-change/sensor-bias pair is either a simple non-identifiability example
or, once a process model, fault set and witness cost are specified, the
established FDI detectability/isolability and sensor-placement problem. A
small fixture can demonstrate one finite pair but cannot convert that into a
new mechanism or general minimum-witness claim.

[KILL] `OFFLINE-REV-NULL` is not a primary direction. A cache without an
updated revocation/freshness fact cannot learn an unseen remote revocation; a
trusted lease/clock, signed status or online update supplies the conventional
baseline. The paired-world proof is a useful protocol boundary but no longer
an independent research contribution.

## Search reset

Do not generate another candidate by combining a Raspberry Pi, a sensor, an
edge constraint, abstention, a two-world counterexample or a known control
mechanism. The next candidate must begin with all of the following from an
accessible real workflow:

1. a repeated actor decision and documented harm from a wrong decision;
2. a source of independent outcome records or a physically defensible truth
   source that remains unavailable to the proposed policy;
3. an operational restriction that prevents the obvious direct witness or
   standard control from being the complete solution; and
4. enough repeated access to collect held-out conditions before proposing a
   method.

## Status

**`START-WIT`, `CAUSE-NULL` and `OFFLINE-REV-NULL`: KILL as primary FYP
directions. No live thesis candidate remains.**
