## Reconciled result

[KILL] `START-WIT` is not a positive FYP mechanism. The independent optical
tachometer/speed witness directly observes the declared shaft-start predicate
and is a low-cost operational baseline; if it can be installed for truth, it
can also be used for a deadline rule at runtime. If it cannot remain installed
in the intended story, the bench proxy has no established transport link to
that field constraint. Separately, prefix decisions, early stopping, rejection
and error-delay costs are established early-time-series-classification methods.

The low-voltage fixture, remount/load holdout and action-loss table remain
valid experimental hygiene, but they do not form a new task, observation or
guarantee. Do not procure a motor, current sensor or accelerometer as a thesis
gate. The fixture may be used later only as a clearly labelled
direct-witness-versus-proxy replication/control.

## Next candidate gate

No candidate is promoted. The next decision is a direct comparison of two
negative-result candidates:

1. `CAUSE-NULL` is the preferred physical-story audit. It must construct
   paired process-change and sensor-bias worlds with equal permitted traces,
   then measure the least independent witness that separates them. It fails if

   short-TTL expiry without a meaningful reset/revocation boundary.

`PIN-WIT` remains HOLD only as a constrained software-only harness
diagnosability benchmark. `GCODE-GATE` and `MODEL-SPEC-NULL` are KILL.
