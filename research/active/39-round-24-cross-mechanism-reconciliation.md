## Decision

No thesis direction is promoted. The divergence packet proposed `TRIG-LEAK`, `PART-ACT`, `PALM-EMB-RET`, and `OFFSYNC-SEM`; the independent validation audit found no positive method claim that survives direct-neighbor, baseline, or feasibility checks.

## Checked conclusions

| Candidate | Reconciled status | Reason | Next gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `VIS-GUIDE` | HOLD/PIVOT | Route-compatible, but guided acquisition and sequential biometric decisions are occupied; only an apparatus-specific all-start completion/time evaluation remains. | Freeze the payment executable, collect every eligible start, then compare one prospective prompt against fixed retake/quality controls. |
| `TRIG-LEAK` | HOLD, validation-only | Traffic camouflage and event-level shaping are direct neighbors; a hard deadline plus downstream action loss is the only residual distinction. | Name a real confidentiality owner and test fixed-clock, constant-rate, event-DP and causal controls at matched bytes/deadline. |
| `PART-ACT` | HOLD, validation-only | Partitioning, deadline scheduling and latency/energy selection are occupied; the remaining endpoint is a finite action-risk benchmark. | Use an exact payload oracle and compare local-only, remote-only, fixed partition, EDF and latency-only controls. |
| `PALM-EMB-RET` | HOLD, high-risk audit | Post-match palm-template lifecycle is a plausible privacy endpoint, but requires consent, lawful storage, reproducible attacker artifacts and a multi-service threat model. | Do not collect or retain biometric data for this claim before ethics, storage and attack authorization are explicit. |
| `OFFSYNC-SEM` | KILL as positive method | Without a signed freshness/revocation witness, opposite remote worlds are locally indistinguishable; with one, standard protocol controls apply. | Retain only as a negative boundary example. |
| `G-ADMIT`, `S-SELECT`, `P-DELETE`, `RAW-JPEG-GATE`, `IR-CAUSE`, `AUDIT-GAP` | KILL as broad thesis / PIVOT as bounded pilot | Direct mechanisms or simple baselines occupy the claim; remaining distinctions are apparatus-specific and may produce only null results. | No new hardware or thesis framing before the stated pilot gates pass. |
