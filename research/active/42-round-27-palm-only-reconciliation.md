## Research boundary

Generic ROI, cropper, retry, gallery calibration, matcher replacement, template privacy, stage-risk estimator, NIR fusion block and PAD classifier remain rejected as thesis mechanisms. Palm, not Raspberry Pi deployment, is the research object; Pi, latency, energy and offline operation are constraints and evaluation variables.
