# Round-16 adaptive compression boundary

## Summary

The new adaptive-sensing/compression search produced no promoted thesis direction. Decoder-side side information from NoIR and deterministic progressive transmission are the only Amber hypotheses, and both are bounded exact-marker action tests rather than new codecs. Task-aware filtering, semantic communication, compressed-domain inference, and RAW/ISP adaptation are already occupied method families.

## Findings

`NOIR-SIDEINFO` asks whether a sequential NoIR frame can reduce RGB transmission bytes at fixed exact-code safety on a shaped local link. `PROG-RET` asks whether a base JPEG/WebP layer can trigger retain versus enhancement/reacquire before full transmission. Both need independent payload labels and must charge capture, registration, encoding, bytes, latency and energy.

Direct neighbors include Yin et al. D-CAN 2025 (decoder-side multi-view compression), TAGIC INFOCOM 2024 (task-guided source-channel coding), the 2025 npj Wireless Technology progressive codec, TFIC EUSIPCO 2025 (OCR-aware coding), Guo ECCV 2024/ICLR 2025 (task-aware/multi-task compression), Ge CVPR 2024 (task-aware video encoder control), and Tu TOMM 2024 (reconstruction-free compressed-domain vision). These occupy generic side-information, progressive, task-aware and compressed-domain claims.

The honest residual is a finite physical endpoint: a purchased Pi RGB/NoIR pair, a static printed marker, a local shaped link, and an exact payload oracle. `ROI-QP` and `DCT-QR` are best retained as negative/engineering controls. `TASK-CODEC-NULL` is a feasible negative protocol: tuned JPEG/ROI may dominate learned codecs on this small task once Pi cost is charged.

## Insights

- A second camera is only useful as compression side information if its registration and transmission cost are included; otherwise `send decoded code` or ROI JPEG is a stronger baseline.
- Progressive stopping has value only when a non-oracle decoder can detect an undecidable result without creating false-retain errors. A full-JPEG or low/high fixed-quality frontier can eliminate it.
- The remaining distinction is endpoint-specific, not algorithmic novelty. A null that simple JPEG or decoded-code transmission dominates is scientifically useful and avoids an unnecessary learned-codec project.

## Strongest objection

NoIR may be unavailable at the receiver or weakly correlated with RGB under transparent-cover/low-light cells. In that case its bytes and registration cost make it strictly worse than ROI JPEG or sending the decoded marker. Progressive confidence may also be overconfident at low quality. These are first-order kill tests, not after-the-fact explanations.
