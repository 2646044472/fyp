# Round 25 Targeted Reconciliation

## Summary

The targeted 2026-09-24 divergence and validation packets agree that no method thesis is promoted. `E-BOUND` is the strongest residual only as a three-stage longitudinal evaluation: enrollment decision, optional confirmation/refusal, and an untouched later full transaction. `PA-CHARGE` is a bounded physical-presentation-to-action audit, not a PAD or payment-security method. `NOIR-SIDEINFO` and `PROG-RET` remain finite matched-cost communication/control benchmarks.

## Findings

`E-BOUND` must freeze the matcher, gallery and threshold, compare best-quality, random, same-session, fixed multi-template, fixed two-session and refusal arms, and count every originally eligible person at a later untouched visit. The key threat is selection bias: conditional accuracy among successfully enrolled users is not the target estimand.

2026-09-24 implementation correction: the current admin enrollment calls `CameraFeed.frame()` five times in a tight loop by default. The feed returns the latest frame, with no fresh-frame-ID wait; stored template metadata lacks per-sample frame/time/session lineage. Five stored features are therefore not proof of five independent physical presentations. Demonstrate distinct presentations and provenance before treating the implementation as an `E-BOUND` data collection path.

`PA-CHARGE` must separate capture failure, ROI failure, PAD decision, 1:N identity, final action and timing/resource outcomes. A physical attack that never reaches the claimed endpoint is an apparatus result, not evidence of a successful defence. No production-payment or security claim is allowed.

`NOIR-SIDEINFO` must charge the second capture, registration, transfer and decoding, and compare against ROI JPEG and code-only controls. `PROG-RET` must use only received bytes and calibration-frozen outputs; payload truth or test-set confidence as an input invalidates the stopping result. A paired-world hidden-suffix construction remains the negative identifiability path.

## Insights

- The most promising residual is a protocol-level longitudinal estimand, not a new model.
- End-to-end action endpoints are scientifically useful only if stage denominators and strong fixed controls are preserved; multiplying component error rates is invalid when stages are dependent.
- Apparatus-specific distinctions can support an empirical boundary result, but do not support a global novelty statement.

## Strongest objection

The exact combination of cross-visit enrollment, later open-set palm transaction and original-eligible-person accounting was not verified in one inspected primary paper. That is a retrieval gap, and the positive claim may still collapse to best-quality or refusal once revisit burden and coverage are charged.

## Open questions

- Can the MVP produce the frozen all-start transaction trace before any longitudinal protocol is attempted?
- Are repeat visits, unknown participants, consent, storage/deletion and a third untouched test visit feasible?
- Does a physical PAI apparatus have authorization and enough independent sources/materials for a full-path audit?
- Does the actual camera/link provide side information that remains useful after charging capture, pairing and transfer costs?
