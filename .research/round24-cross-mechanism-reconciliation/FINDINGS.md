# Round 24 Cross-Mechanism Reconciliation

## Summary

The 2026-09-24 divergence and validation packets agree that no edge-computing thesis direction is currently promoted. `VIS-GUIDE` is the only route-compatible Palm-side lead, and only as an apparatus-specific completion/time evaluation after a frozen payment executable and all-start trace. `TRIG-LEAK` and `PART-ACT` are independent validation-only HOLDs with explicit fixed-clock/fixed-partition controls. `PALM-EMB-RET` is a high-risk consented privacy audit, not a ready method claim. `OFFSYNC-SEM` is a negative indistinguishability control.

## Findings

The divergence side generated four cross-mechanism candidates. Traffic camouflage and event-level shaping occupy the generic `TRIG-LEAK` mechanism; only a named hard deadline, downstream notice loss and matched byte/energy budget remain to be tested. Adaptive partitioning, deadline-aware serving and data-aware scheduling occupy the generic `PART-ACT` mechanism; only a finite wrong-action versus p95 completion/energy benchmark remains. Pre-capture and on-device visual privacy work does not close the post-match palm-template lifecycle endpoint, but the latter requires lawful consent, a reproducible attacker and explicit storage/deletion semantics.

The validation side found high collision for broad positive claims. Palm ROI, quality recapture and sequential accept/reject/continue are established. Cheap-sentinel sampling, risk-aware probing, active diagnosis, screen/confirm acquisition, RAW processing, adaptive exposure, cross-device palm recognition and protected-template linkability each have direct neighbors. The remaining apparatus distinctions are retrieval gaps or bounded empirical evaluations, not evidence of novelty.

## Insights

- The project should separate execution commitment from research promotion: the Pi Palm Payment MVP is justified by the user route and engineering endpoint, not by a new algorithm claim.
- A candidate survives only if its action and independent endpoint cannot be reproduced by a fixed policy or direct-state read. Apparatus specificity alone is insufficient.
- Negative results are credible only with independent labels, matched costs, denominators and a predeclared stopping rule; otherwise they are feasibility notes.

## Strongest objection

The exact combination of a Pi payment executable, hard deadline, action loss, or post-match palm-template retention may not appear in one inspected paper. That is a bounded retrieval gap, not a novelty result. The current records still lack the Pi live trace, ethics/consent package, independent physical reference and some full-text advisor-paper chains.

## Open questions

- Does the frozen Palm Payment executable produce a measurable stage failure and enough all-start opportunities for a prospective `VIS-GUIDE` evaluation?
- Can a real owner define `TRIG-LEAK` confidentiality, deadline, byte budget and attacker vantage before any implementation work?
- Can `PART-ACT` obtain an exact payload oracle and matched local/remote energy accounting without becoming a scheduler replication?
- Is there ethics approval and a lawful retention/deletion/attacker protocol for `PALM-EMB-RET`?
