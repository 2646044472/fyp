# Two Raspberry Pi next-direction decision

## Summary

The two boards expand the apparatus, but do not by themselves create a research contribution. The strongest current plan is staged: build a reliable Palm Reg demo, run a short RGB/NoIR physical action pilot, and promote a thesis claim only if a held-out result beats fixed two-shot and scalar-quality baselines. Federated continual learning and cooperative perception remain HOLD or replication/negative-benchmark options.

## Findings

The active research directory has no locked thesis direction. The palm branch is an engineering/runtime baseline; generic adaptive camera selection, RAW robustness, progressive transmission, cooperative perception, split-inference privacy and board-agreement mechanisms have direct neighboring work. A broad two-Pi collaboration or edge-AI-on-Raspberry-Pi thesis is therefore not supported.

The live gate is a finite physical observation study using a printed non-personal marker, an independently stored payload, pre-assigned physical cells, and actions such as retain, reacquire, and unknown. Compare RGB, NoIR plus IR, fixed two-shot, and simple brightness/blur rules under matched capture budget; hold out lamp, cover and remount cells. A positive result is only a bounded physical action-rank effect. A null, non-identifiability result, or fixed-policy win should close the branch.

The best non-optical alternative is FCL-PI-NB: frozen FedAvg/FedProx/replay/distillation controls on a small non-personal incremental stream across Pi 4 and Pi 5, measuring accuracy, forgetting, bytes, time, thermal behavior and energy. Current evidence makes this a reproducible small-device boundary or negative benchmark, not a new optimizer. COOP-MARK is feasible as an extension of the physical pilot, but board agreement is not independent truth. SPLIT-LEAK-AUDIT is suitable as a bounded privacy appendix, not a new defense.

## Insights

- The boards are most valuable as controlled instrumentation and replication units, not claimed novelty.
- One fixture can test IR value, physical-vs-digital transfer, two-view cooperation and link cost without committing to a new model.
- Fixed two-shot is the main adversary; if it wins, the project still has a clean negative result and a working Palm Reg demo.

## Strongest objection

The physical pilot may be only static-marker characterization because RGB/NoIR ordering can remain stable and fixed two-shot may be Pareto-optimal. FCL may likewise reproduce known algorithms without a distinct mechanism.

## Open questions

- Is a second RGB camera available for Pi 5, and what are the exact camera/IR revisions?
- Can a rigid fixture, transparent cover/occluder and repeatable lighting be obtained?
- Does a short pilot show a held-out action-rank inversion against fixed two-shot and scalar-quality controls?
- If null, should the project choose a negative physical-transfer paper or a systems FCL benchmark?
