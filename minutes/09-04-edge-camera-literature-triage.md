# Edge-Camera Literature Triage (2026-09-04)

## Decision investigated

Which story-first `edge + camera` candidates remain worth formalizing after a
direct-neighbor check, given that palm is out of scope and additional hardware
may be purchased.

## Direct-neighbor results

### [KILL] Plain analog-gauge reader on an edge device

Do not frame a thesis as reading an analog gauge with a Pi, a camera, a new
detector, a VLM, or a reconstruction network. Direct work already includes an
IoT gauge-monitoring system, a 2025 edge-device gauge reader, ALMA-IA's 2025
edge-device gauge system, and a 2026 occlusion-reconstruction method.

Residual only: a bounded decision problem about when a displayed reading can be
released to a non-safety record, recaptured, or reviewed under independently
logged physical optical conditions. The technical claim must be about the
release decision or digital-to-physical evaluation boundary, not needle
estimation.

### [KILL] Plain edge insect detector or trigger

Do not frame a thesis as detecting/counting insects with a Pi or using a tiny
CNN as an on-device trigger. 2025 work already covers ultra-lightweight
on-device insect-camera triggers and resource-constrained insect segmentation
and counting.

Residual only: a controlled false-absence / manual-review problem under
nighttime IR capture, where a non-detection cannot be treated as a valid empty
record without independent trap truth and a finite review budget.

### [KILL] Broad claim that digital corruptions do not represent physical ones

Do not claim that synthetic or digital camera corruptions are generally invalid
for real-world robustness. Agnihotri et al. (CVPR 2025 SynData4CV Workshop)
already evaluates synthetic-versus-real corruptions and reports high mean
performance correlation with condition-specific exceptions.

Residual only: a finite physical-camera deployment decision study in which
digital tests choose among named actions and the study measures the resulting
real-world action loss. It must not claim a general theorem or broad benchmark
verdict.

| GAUGE-RELEASE | Technician records a non-critical analog gauge reading. | Do digital image tests select the correct `release / recapture / review` action under glass glare, camera angle, condensation, and remount changes? | Low-voltage panel-meter value generated by an encoder/MCU or independently logged digital meter. | PIVOT: story strong, ordinary gauge-reading claim killed. |

| NIGHT-ABSENCE | Ecological/greenhouse operator records a nighttime trap observation. | Under IR capture and a review budget, when may an edge device record an empty trap rather than request/review another observation? | Blinded manual trap count or controlled placement count. | HOLD: strong story, but data/seasonality and direct monitoring literature are risks. |
| ACTIVE-IR-VOI | Technician inspects a label, gauge, or marker behind a cover. | Is one extra controlled IR observation worth its latency/energy compared with fixed recapture, fixed two-shot, or review? | The host task's independent truth. | HOLD: an intervention-value problem, not IR enhancement. |

## Current conclusion

The next candidate must be defined by a real record-release decision plus an
independent truth path. Camera reading, insect detection, or a Pi deployment
is an apparatus component, not the claimed contribution.
