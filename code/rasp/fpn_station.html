Reading the metrics and camera settings
  • ROI share of frame is the proportion of the frame covered by the green ROI. Use it to reproduce distance while leaving space around the palm.
  • ROI brightness is the median gray level of the 128 × 128 ROI. Zero is black and 255 is white. Check for local glare even when the median is in range.
  • Relative sharpness measures variation in edge strength. Compare the reading and the fine creases at different distances while keeping the hand, lighting and pose fixed.
  • ROI contrast is the standard deviation of gray levels. Fine creases, glare and broad shadows all raise it; zoom in to tell them apart.

Use the size and brightness ranges to adjust the setup, then inspect the actual creases in the original image.

If the palm has a bright spot, move the light to one side or diffuse it, then check the fine creases.

Keep the camera-to-palm distance steady and pause for focus. Tilt the same hand slightly, then hold it still after each movement.
